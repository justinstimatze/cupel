# Glossary — cupel vocabulary, ruthlessly small

The canonical reference for cupel's working vocabulary.

**The discipline:** this doc lists what survives. Anything not here gets rewritten in plain prose where it appears. The point is to prune, not to relabel inflation.

When adding a new term, the bar: name the *distinct work* the term does that plain prose can't. If the term is just compressing two existing words into a hyphenated form (X-register ≈ "in the X mode of"), prefer the prose.

---

## 1. Foundation — the engine substrate

**engine.** A wish-fulfillment mechanism with three slots. cupel's unit of analysis. The twenty confirmed engines: *mastery*, *apotheosis*, *belonging*, *being-desired*, *redemption*, *order/legibility*, *impunity*, *purity*, *security*, *liberation/autonomy*, *caretaking/being-needed*, *repricing*, *legacy*, *abundance*, *virtue of defeat*, *homecoming/reunion*, *unleashing*, *the double life*, *wound*, *recognition*. Each is defined with its slots, wish-valence guard, specimens, and counterfeit on its `/engines/<slug>/` page.

**wound class.** The catalog's first **gratify-by-refusing-to-give** axis. The protagonist carries a constitutive wound; slot-3 is *preserving the wound through every cure offered*. The wound stays the bearer's, which produces a class-level consumption-deficit. Four branches by the wound's origin: **self-administered** (the Underground Man), **witnessed** (Holden's Allie-wound), **received** (Jude's history), **received-then-preserved** (Frollo's obsession; Humbert's Annabel-prototype). Discrimination guard: would the bearer accept cure-without-cost? Yes → wound-as-backstory (engine is something else); no → wound candidate.

**bead.** The work-card's concentrated distillation. What cupellation leaves after the dross is burned off — the metallurgical metaphor the project name (*cupel*) draws from. Opens every work card's `## The reading` section as a single paragraph naming the work's spine: setting, engines, and payoff. The catalog is the running record of beads extracted.

**slot 1.** The condition the engine addresses — the lack, the unmet need, the indebted state. For *mastery*: the unskilled condition. For *redemption*: the real debt.

**slot 2.** The mechanism that pays the cost — the substantive work that earns the slot-3 payoff. For *mastery*: actual practice over time. For *redemption*: the costly atonement actually made.

**slot 3.** The payout — the wish delivered. For *mastery*: standing capacity. For *redemption*: forgiven / clean.

**counterfeit.** Slot 3 claimed without slot 2 paid. The engine's payout delivered (or appearing to be delivered) without the substantive cost. The cupel's central diagnostic move: identify the slot-2-counterfeit and you've identified the work's load-bearing dishonesty.

**slot-2 deficit.** The specific gap where slot 2 is missing or faked. Naming the deficit precisely is the discipline that separates a slot-test from a vibe judgment.

**texture (vs engine).** What a work *delivers as supply-side experience*, distinct from the wish the audience consumes the work *for*. Awe, wonder, the sublime, spectacle, dread, suspense — all textures, not engines. A texture *rides along* on whichever engine actually drives consumption: awe in *Jurassic Park* rides on order/legibility-antagonist-mode (the deep-past rendered legible); spectacle in *Avatar* rides on belonging (the Na'vi tribe); suspense in thrillers rides on whichever wish the bearer is pursuing. The **demand-side test** is the discriminator: an engine is what people consume the work *for* (a wish about their life); texture is what the work delivers *as* (an experience cultivated). Awe-rich content is sought at niche scale by people who seek the cultivation; engines drive mass consumption. Apply when a slot-test feels coerced: if the slot you're tagging is the work's texture rather than the wish driving its en-masse consumption, you're confusing texture for engine. Worked example in [Jurassic Park](/cupel/works/jurassic-park/).

**wish-valence guard.** The rule that bars the shortcut: the payoff must carry its real backing (slot 2), and the protagonist must actually *want* what the engine delivers. The guard rejects specimens that fill the bare slots but route a different wish (Scrooge fills unleashing's slots but is redemption — his trigger is a dreaded reckoning, not a welcomed license). Load-bearing across the falsification log; the *bearer-realizable line* is the wish-valence guard's general form (a wish has a counterfeit when its payoff is bearer-realizable; lacks one when externally-adjudicated). Per [Falsification log](/cupel/theory/falsification-log/). **Structural substrate ([The interpreter and the engines](/cupel/theory/interpreter-and-engines/)):** the guard is the *interpreter-coherence test* — would the bearer's left-hemisphere-interpreter self-story survive the wish being granted? Wound bearers fail it (the wound IS the load-bearing structure); not-wound trauma specimens (Moshfegh, Machado, Norwegian Wood, Maus) pass it (the interpreter has built an alternative coherence the wound's resolution is compatible with). This is *why* the guard is binding, not just *that* it is.

## 2. Slot-test outcomes — what a candidate engine resolves to

When an engine is slot-tested, it resolves into one of these categories. Documented in the README and [Falsification log](/cupel/theory/falsification-log/).

**confirmed.** The candidate cleared every gate: a clean specimen checked against the page, a wish-valence guard, and a distinct counterfeit shown. Joins the engine table.

**candidate.** Promising — a clean specimen may exist, but at least one gate (guard, counterfeit, isolation from neighbors) is not yet closed. Tracked in [Falsification log](/cupel/theory/falsification-log/).

**composite.** The candidate's wish decomposes into existing engines, so it is not its own engine. *Fame* = being-desired + repricing.

**solvent.** A real wish whose counterfeit dissolves into other engines, so it is named honestly yet kept out of the engine table. The shared shape: an *externally-adjudicated verdict the bearer cannot self-confer*. *Election* (chosen), *vindication* (proven-right), *justice/comeuppance* (desert-served). The bearer-realizable line is the rule.

**vehicle.** Not a wish but a *delivery device* — *transport* carries you to a new world, *transformation* is the crucible by which other engines render their payoff. Catalogued for clarity but not a tag.

**Specimen-resolution result-types (i)/(ii)/(iii)/(iv) + split-result + scaffold.** A second resolution layer below engine-resolution: when a *specific work* is slot-tested against the confirmed engine set, it resolves to one of these. (i) engine-present-run-dark-still-delivered; (ii) engine-shape-present-in-character-run-to-expose; (iii) no-engine-isolates; (iv) engine-of-a-kind-the-catalog-wasn't-reading (the surfacing-case for new candidate engines). **split-result** = character-engine layer fills cleanly while reader-engine layer remains a resister OR runs a consumption-counterfeit at a different engine (Lolita post-2026-06-05). **scaffold** = the work distributes engine-bundle elements across multiple bearers rather than co-locating them in one bearer; the bundle is not a unitary cluster but a recurring structural element-set that hosts different engines in different works (Hugo's Notre-Dame for the masked-vigilante scaffold). Full treatment + decision tree at [Result-types](/cupel/theory/result-types/).

**content-layer ↔ consumption-layer.** The on-page material an engine runs on vs the audience-pose consuming the work. Distinct readings of the same text. Lolita runs wound substantively at the content-layer (Humbert as bearer) and unleashing-counterfeit at the consumption-layer (prurient-reader-mode). The wound class's reader-engine dual-use signature is the canonical case.

**character-engine layer ↔ reader-engine layer.** A finer-grained restatement of content-layer / consumption-layer, used when a single work runs engines at both layers and they need to be tracked separately (Gravity's Rainbow runs wound at the character-engine layer via Slothrop and order/legibility-antagonist-mode at the reader-engine layer via the novel's form). **Reader-engine** = the engine running at the consumption-layer; **audience-pose** = the actor's stance through which that engine is inhabited. The two are related but distinct: the reader-engine names *what mechanism runs*; the audience-pose names *how the reader takes it up*.

**consumption-counterfeit.** A counterfeit running at the consumption-layer rather than at the content-layer. The audience-pose engine the work supplies as substitute when the content-layer engine cannot be transmitted directly. The wound class's class-level dual-use signature — every wound specimen carries a consumption-counterfeit at a different engine because the wound's gratify-by-refusing-to-give axis creates a consumption-deficit (the audience cannot share the bearer's wound) that a neighboring engine's counterfeit-shape fills based on the work's surface texture.

**engine-transmission routes.** The structural account of *how* an engine reaches the audience-pose, generalizing the wound class-finding. The impunity engine surfaces three routes: **route 1** (cult-text frame-only → vindication-solvent substitution; the audience cannot inhabit the bearer's slot-3), **route 2** (non-fictional bearer-realizable tactics → engine direct; the audience operationally inhabits), **route 3** (fictional worldbuilding-real → engine via POV-inhabitation while reading; four sub-variants A invulnerability / B gadget-training / C consequence-reversibility / D mutation-hybridity). Routes 1 + 2 can run **co-loaded** at the same engine-position (Kiyosaki RDPD — both frame-vindication and bearer-realizable-tactics simultaneously; partitions cluster-internal structure rather than just classifying specimens). The general principle: transmission depends on whether the audience-pose can structurally inhabit the engine's slot-3; engines whose slot-3 is bearer-specific (wound) cannot transmit directly and substitute a neighboring engine or solvent.

## 3. Specimen grades — what "we know" about a work

These are *evidence levels*, listed weakest to strongest. Used in card frontmatter (`backing:`).

**wikipedia-grounded.** Claims about the work derived from secondary sources (Wikipedia, reviews, critical literature). No primary-text verification. The lowest grade above pure assertion. Honest about its limits.

**~ reviewed.** Light-engagement card. Claims about engines / cluster placement made on the basis of reading the work *generally*, without verbatim slot-anchoring. Faster than slot-proven; suitable for triage; not durable for theoretical load-bearing.

**slot-proven.** Verbatim quotes from the primary text confirm that slots 1 / 2 / 3 of each claimed engine fill. The discipline-anchored grade. The `works/` directory holds all work cards; each card's frontmatter `backing` field marks it as `slot-proven` (carries a verbatim-cited `## The evidence` section) or `reviewed` (lighter card without the evidence section).

**canonical text.** A work that *defines* the cluster — the founder-canon specimen against which derivatives are read. Carnegie 1936 for self-help, Thiel 2014 for startup-canon pure-counterfeit, Hubbard 1950 for cult.

## 4. Bundle — the positive-pole co-occurrence

**bundle.** A co-occurrence of two-or-more engines running *enabling* in a recognizable cultural formation. The positive-pole counterpart of the counterfeit cluster. The *romance bundle* runs being-desired + abundance + a powerful partner (Twilight, ACOTAR, *A Little Princess* is the abundance-spine version). The *quest bundle* runs mastery + belonging + the heroic-deed register. The *bearing-witness bundle* runs virtue-of-defeat + the-double-life-of-the-witness-once-removed + care-for-the-record (Spiegelman's *Maus*, Machado's *In the Dream House*) — the dark-bundle specimen for testimony-at-the-archival register. The *reckoning-without-redemption bundle* (Robinson's *Lila*) is another dark-bundle named specimen. Full catalog in [Bundle-shape catalog](/cupel/theory/bundle-shape-catalog/).

Distinct from *cluster*: a cluster runs counterfeit, a bundle runs enabling. Distinct from *composite*: a composite is a single wish that decomposes into engines; a bundle is multiple wishes co-occurring.

**bright bundle ↔ dark bundle.** The valence dimension within the bundle catalog (per dimension 3 of the pole-dimension list below). Bright-bundle wish-fulfillment (the romance bundle, the quest bundle) vs dark-bundle (the reckoning-without-redemption bundle).

## 5. Cluster — the unit above engine

**counterfeit cluster.** A co-occurrence of two-or-more engines running counterfeit in a self-reinforcing structural shape that recurs as a recognizable cultural formation. Distinct from a *bundle* (positive-pole co-occurrence) and from a *single-engine counterfeit* (one engine running falsely in isolation). The mutual-reinforcement structure is what makes the cluster harder to dissolve than any single counterfeit-leg.

**leg (of a cluster).** An engine that the cluster runs. Written as **X-leg** where X is a named engine: *mastery-leg*, *apotheosis-leg*, *impunity-leg*, *order/legibility-leg*. Load-bearing because the cluster catalog tracks which legs each cluster has and how they reinforce. Do not use X-leg outside this cluster-component sense.

**N-leg (count compound).** Written as **N-leg** where N is the count: *four-leg specimen* (a work running four cluster legs), *six-leg cluster*, *seven-leg compound*. Compact shorthand for "fills N legs"; load-bearing because the cluster-counts are a recurring axis of comparison across dossiers (cult-cluster four-leg vs Jonestown-compound seven-leg).

**gravitational center.** The cross-cluster repeating leg-triplet: *apotheosis + impunity + order/legibility*. The three legs that appear across every recruitment-cluster mapped so far. The cluster's *closed-loop of recruitment*: elevation justifies exemption; exemption necessary because revealed truth differs from conventional rules; elevation is *evidence* of the revealed truth.

**primary cluster axis (three-way).** *Recruitment* / *narrative* / *compression*. Recruitment-clusters inject the gravitational-center loop and recruit members. Narrative-clusters run story-form gravitational centers (romance-trap). Compression-clusters package a wisdom-tradition into consumption-deliverable (wisdom-tradition-compression). Different gravitational-center shapes, different signature slot-2-deficits.

**compound cluster.** A single specimen running two or more clusters simultaneously (NXIVM = cult ∩ self-help ∩ MLM; Jonestown = cult ∩ protected-world). Documented in [Cluster catalog](/cupel/theory/cluster-catalog/) § Compound clusters.

**bridge specimen.** A work whose function is to *pipeline* readers/viewers between two clusters (e.g. wellness → alt-right; pop-philosophy → manosphere). Bridge specimens are the cluster-bridging finding's operational unit.

## 6. Dimensions and poles — the discipline this glossary exists to fix

"Pole" had been used as if it referred to a single fixed dimension, but multiple distinct dimensions were in play. The fix: **"pole" is a useful word only when the dimension is named.** Each dimension below has a name, two endpoints, and a defined scope of application.

### Dimension 1 — textual-attitude (the one "pole" is reserved for)

**partial-refusal ↔ pure-counterfeit.** The dimension along which a canonical text *positions itself toward the cluster's slot-2 deficit*.

- **pure-counterfeit pole.** The text's load-bearing claim *is* the slot-2 deficit — the framework works *by* installing slot 3 without the slot-2 cost. Hill 1937, Thiel 2014, Hubbard 1950.
- **partial-refusal pole.** The text *names* the slot-2 deficit explicitly and proposes a substantive replacement — the technique's effectiveness depends on the slot-2 work being done honestly. Carnegie 1936 (local-principle register), Covey 1989 (structural-frame register), Ries 2011 (structural-frame register).

This is the dimension the *bistable-braid* finding lives on. **The word "pole" in cupel dossiers, used without qualifier, refers to this dimension.** Any other use must name its dimension explicitly.

### Dimension 2 — value-flow (per-specimen judgment)

**enabling ↔ counterfeit** (the running's grade). Used in dual-use reads to grade *a specimen's actual effect on the reader*. Distinct from dimension 1: a partial-refusal-pole text can still run counterfeit at the consumption layer (Covey-as-MBA-shelf-identity, Ries-as-cargo-cult), and a pure-counterfeit-pole text can still be enabling for some readers (Hill as motivational scaffold).

Do not use "enabling-pole" / "counterfeit-pole" as compound forms in dossier prose. Write "the running is enabling" / "the running is counterfeit." The dimension stays useful; the compound form is fluency-noise.

### Dimension 3 — bundle valence (positive-pole work)

**bright ↔ dark** (within the bundle catalog, [Bundle-shape catalog](/cupel/theory/bundle-shape-catalog/)). Applies to *bundles* (positive-pole co-occurrences), not clusters. Used to distinguish bright-bundle wish-fulfillment (the romance bundle, the quest bundle) from dark-bundle (the reckoning-without-redemption bundle).

This dimension is local to the bundle catalog. Outside that context, "bright pole" and "dark pole" should be rewritten in prose.

### Dimensions that were used but get pruned

- **prevention pole.** Used in [Dual-use log](/cupel/theory/counterfeit-catalog/). Rewrite in prose: "the prevention-oriented running of the engine" or similar.
- **solvent pole.** Used once in [Falsification log](/cupel/theory/falsification-log/). Drop; rewrite in prose.
- **running pole.** Used once in [Going Clear](/cupel/works/going-clear/). Drop; rewrite in prose.

## 7. Register — when load-bearing, when not

**register.** A specific structural layer at which a discipline / mechanism / counterfeit operates. Examples that are load-bearing:

- *local-principle register* — Carnegie's flattery-vs-appreciation discipline runs inside a single principle.
- *structural-frame register* — Covey's Character-Ethic-vs-Personality-Ethic is the book's organizing frame; Ries's anti-mythology framing is the book's organizing claim.
- *trainable-craft register* — Clear's atomic-habits structure delivers as trainable practice without explicit slot-2-counterfeit naming.
- *consumption-layer register* — what happens when the work is read by mass audiences, distinct from the work's text-internal register.
- *care-for-the-record register* — caretaking running at the historical-trauma / archival-silence layer rather than dyadic-personal (Spiegelman's *Maus* preserving Vladek's account; Machado's *In the Dream House* preserving queer-domestic-abuse testimony). Distinct from caretaking-of-a-needy-other; contrasts with the absence-of-record the testimony fills. Named in [Bundle-shape catalog](/cupel/theory/bundle-shape-catalog/) § bearing-witness bundle.

"Register" is load-bearing **when it names a specific layer the analysis discriminates** (as in the self-help cluster's three-register finding: local-principle / structural-frame / trainable-craft). It is fluency-noise when used as a generic-purpose modifier ("at the X register" where X is just the noun being analyzed; *mastery-register*, *apotheosis-register*, *counterfeit-register* — these collapse to "in the X mode of" and should be rewritten).

Heuristic for keeping a *X-register* compound: can you name the *contrasting* register the dimension implies? If "the apotheosis-register" doesn't have a named not-apotheosis-register on the other side, the term is doing no discriminating work.

## 8. Mode and sub-mode — similar discipline

**mode.** Used in cluster-axis terms (*recruitment-mode*, *narrative-mode*, *compression-mode*). Load-bearing because the cluster catalog's primary axis is three-way.

**sub-mode.** Load-bearing in the outside-critique dossier work — distinguishing *journalist-distance / former-member-reckoning / outer-participant-rescuer* sub-modes of the outside-critique-position. Three documented sub-modes, distinct discrimination work.

**antagonist-mode.** An engine running in *opposition / refusal / deconstruction* rather than enabling. The contrasting mode is the unmarked enabling-mode (engine runs to deliver the wish). Load-bearing where it names *which specific engine* opposes — `apotheosis-antagonist-mode` (Watchmen, Evangelion: the work dramatizes apotheosis-counterfeit's catastrophic success); `order/legibility-antagonist-mode` (Blood Meridian, Gravity's Rainbow: totalizing legibility as the work's substantive antagonism); `impunity-antagonist-mode` (Watchmen at the multi-bearer register). Distinct from dimension-2 value-flow (enabling ↔ counterfeit): an engine can run antagonist-mode AND be the work's enabling running, because the work earns its enabling status *by* the deconstruction.

Generic *X-mode* compound forms (*god-mode*, *failure-mode*) — rewrite in prose unless they similarly name a contrasting mode.

## 9. Findings — named structural results

Findings get a name when they recur enough that future work cross-references them. Each finding's full statement lives in a `theory/` doc; the entry here is the one-paragraph plain-language form.

**bistable-braid.** Some clusters have *two parallel canonical-text traditions* — one running honestly (partial-refusal pole), one running as a recruitment pitch (pure-counterfeit pole) — both surviving and propagating from the same founding moment. Carnegie 1936 (honest) and Hill 1937 (counterfeit) braided the self-help cluster; Ries 2011 (honest) and Thiel 2014 (counterfeit) braided startup-canon. Full proposal: [Foundational-moment bistability](/cupel/theory/foundational-moment-bistability/).

**cluster-internal participant-refusal.** A text inside a cluster that names what the cluster's other texts skip — the slot-2 deficit, in glossary terms — and refuses to make the same skip itself. Manson *Subtle Art* refuses the self-help cluster's positivity script; Brown *Daring Greatly* refuses its bootstrapping script; Doyle *Untamed* refuses its earlier-self's script. The catalog once treated this as a strong "this text is honest" signal; the Doyle slot-test refined that — sometimes the refused position is just *replaced by the same engine running somewhere else in the text*, not structurally refused.

**cluster-external systemic-refusal.** A work outside a cluster deploys the *same engine* the cluster runs in counterfeit form *against* the cluster, with falsifiable method. Distinct from *outside-critique pole* (a position *within* the cluster's pole-spectrum, taken by a text that names the cluster as religion/cult/etc.): cluster-external systemic-refusal sits *adjacent to* the cluster, not on its spectrum. Carreyrou *Bad Blood* (startup-canon-cluster engines mastery + impunity + order/legibility deployed via investigative journalism against Theranos). Documented in [Bundle-shape catalog](/cupel/theory/bundle-shape-catalog/).

**pole-distribution shapes.** Within a cluster, the canonical texts spread across the partial-refusal ↔ pure-counterfeit dimension in one of a small set of shapes. A text can hold the *same pole at every engine* in the cluster (the cleanest case — DiAngelo *White Fragility* uniformly partial-refusal across the systemic-critique cluster's engines). A text can *mix poles across engines* — partial-refusal at some, pure-counterfeit at others (Kendi *How to Be an Antiracist*). A text can sit *inside the cluster's project but refuse its own myth-temptations* (Coates *The Message* refusing vindicationist mythology while staying anti-racist). These are the structural intermediates that fill in between bistable-braid's two endpoints.

**dissolution-mechanism pattern.** The closed 5-pattern typology in the cult-cluster work for how cluster-canonical instances end: *demand-imposed exit*, *gradual departure*, *failed-prophecy reckoning*, *catastrophic collapse*, *scripted external-state catalysis*. Load-bearing closure; per `memory/keep-gates-hard-name-new-categories`.

## 10. Conventions — small, mechanical

- **`works/<slug>.md`** — one file per work; the analytical reading and (when slot-proven) the verbatim evidence both live inside it. Tier marked by the frontmatter `backing` field (`slot-proven` or `reviewed`).
- **`theory/<topic>.md`** — methodological / theoretical / catalog work.
- **In dossiers, citation parens contain only verbatim quoted spans, structural markers (Ch. N, l. N, S2E3, Speaker:), and cross-refs.** No LLM-paraphrase in cite position. Per `memory/quotes-only-no-paraphrase`. Enforced by `cupel quotes-audit`.
- **In-copyright sources cite bibliographically only** (no "held in refs", no "verified verbatim" boilerplate). Per `memory/dossier-citation-minimal`.
- **spine vs rider / also-runs** — the role-tag a dossier assigns to each engine it identifies. *Spine* = the engine the work is built around; *also-runs* = secondary engines that fill but don't carry. *Rider* is a synonym for *also-runs* preferred in prose when the secondary engine is *bundled with* the spine in a recognizable formation (abundance *rides on* being-desired in the romance bundle). In dossier engine-list tags use *also-runs*; in analytical prose either is fine.
