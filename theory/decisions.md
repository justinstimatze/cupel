# Decisions — what was chosen and why

The structural calls that shaped the project. Each was contested; the record matters because the shape they produced is loadbearing.

## Done-state

Floor: a lexicon cluster of the engines as reusable lenses, gated on being able to bring primary citations. Ambition: a defender / media-literacy artifact. An essay is a fallback, not the goal.

## Creator tool rejected

A diagnostic for people who *build* wish-fulfillment (novelists, ad and pitch writers, organizers) feels exploitive — it hands the engine to whoever runs it, including the demagogue calibrating the trigger and the marketer tuning the badge. The defender artifact hands the same lens to the audience. Same analysis, inverted ethics, decided by who holds it.

## Form: Bechdel-style classification, not an Honest-Trailers roast

A classification reads as a fact about the work — the reader can dispute the reading without feeling mocked. The roast register walks straight into the spell-breaking problem: the audience for a wish does not want the wish dunked on. The Bechdel discipline also forces the gates down to checkable slot + citation.

## Name: cupel

Cupellation separates the precious bead from the absorbed dross. *reagent*, *touchstone*, *scholia*, and *shibboleth* all collided hard with existing software.

## Term of art: *the counterfeit*

Each engine's dark use is named **the counterfeit**: it grants the slot-3 identity while skipping the slot-2 cost, so it is the *badge minus its backing* (face value, no reserve). Each engine has a **genuine** form (the wish earned through the real work) and a **counterfeit** (the recruiter selling the same gratification at a discount). Chosen over "dual-use recruitment face" (a mouthful) and "the grift" (which implies a *knowing* con — many counterfeits are sincere-but-corrupt). "Dual-use" is the property (one machine, two uses); "the counterfeit" is the name for the dark face itself.
