# Held-back catalog — slot-1 conditions of being-stuck, per engine

**The move.** Structurally most parallel to the hole catalog in [Backings inventory](/cupel/theory/backings/). For each engine, name the **slot-1 held-back states** (the conditions of dispossession or stuck-ness the engine releases the protagonist *from*) currently represented in the catalog, and the kinds of stuck-ness that aren't.

The backings inventory asked: "what costs-of-the-wish vary by culture?" The held-back inventory asks: "what *conditions of being-stuck* does the catalog assume are universal but aren't?"

**Why this matters.** Engine reads have been culturally generalizable at the *wish* level (everyone wants belonging, freedom, recognition). But the *held-back* — the specific stuck-state the wish releases from — is often a function of the culture and era the catalog over-represents (Western 19c domestic, classical Greek epic, modern American mass-market). Naming the holes points at works that center the *conditions of stuck-ness* the catalog under-represents.

The catalog is per-engine; specimens listed are the slot-1 representations currently in `works/`, and the holes are conditions that should be representable but aren't.

## liberation/autonomy

**Present held-backs:**
- Individual social-imposed-role (Nora's marital "doll" role, Doll's House)
- Legal bondage (Douglass's slavery)
- Internal entanglement / attachment to results (Gita)
- Comfort-without-fit / vocational dissonance (Psalm)
- Engineered Sameness as dystopian system (The Giver)
- Wanting-itself / craving (Dhammapada)
- Striving / contention (Tao Te Ching)
- Assent to what isn't yours (Aurelius, Epictetus)
- Extractive civilisation-virus / displacement from land (Sand Talk)

**Holes — conditions of being-stuck the catalog doesn't represent:**
- **Generational stagnation** — the immigrant's grandchild who can't quite leave the family business; the middle-aged who never broke out and now can't see how. Distinct from individual-imposed-role (Doll's House) because the imposition is *generational and ambient*. Possibly: Jeffrey Eugenides's *Middlesex*, Min Jin Lee's *Pachinko*.
- **Climate-driven displacement** — a newly-emerging genre: the held-back is the home that is becoming uninhabitable. The liberation is involuntary, forward. Specimens: parts of *The Ministry for the Future* (Robinson), *American War* (Akkad).
- **Capitalism-as-structural-cage** — distinct from Marxist liberation specimens because the worker can't *see how to step out* — the cage is naturalized. Mark Fisher's *Capitalist Realism* (theory); some Sally Rooney.
- **Digital-attention-economy capture** — the algorithm-captured self who feels constrained but cannot articulate what's holding them. Distinct from Aurelius's "assent to what isn't yours" because the externality is engineered and addictive. Specimens: Jia Tolentino's *Trick Mirror*, Jenny Odell's *How to Do Nothing*.
- **Postcolonial nation-condition** — the held-back state of a country that won independence but inherits a structure designed to extract from it. Achebe, Adichie, Mahfouz.
- **Late-modern bureaucratic-cage** — Kafka's *Trial* runs this but as antagonist; modern-administrative-state fiction less represented.

## being-desired

**Present held-backs:**
- Modern teenage ordinariness (Bella, Twilight)
- Plain plain-ness (Ana, Fifty Shades)
- Poor mortal huntress (Feyre, ACOTAR)
- Chronically-injuring disabled body (Violet, Fourth Wing)

**Holes:**
- **Middle-aged unwantedness** — most being-desired specimens are 16-25. The held-back of mid-life invisibility (the divorced woman, the "old" man) is its own dispossession. Specimens: some Anne Tyler, Elizabeth Strout's *Olive Kitteridge*.
- **Elderly unwantedness** — the catalog has zero specimens.
- **Post-trauma sexual being-desired** — the held-back is not ordinariness but injury; the wanting must navigate it. Some Mary Karr, some Carmen Maria Machado.
- **Queer-in-religious-context being-desired** — the closet as held-back layered onto ordinariness; the desire's stakes shift. Hollinghurst's *Line of Beauty*, much LGBTQ memoir.
- **Asexual / aromantic being-desired** — the held-back is *the desire itself* refusing to fit conventional shapes. *Loveless* (Oseman).
- **Polyamorous being-desired** — the engine running across more than one desirer. *More Than Two* (Veaux & Rickert) as the framework text; some Janice Hardy.

## belonging

**Present held-backs:**
- Placeless human infant (Mowgli)
- Cupboard-under-the-stairs orphan (Harry)
- Quest-companions across difference (Fellowship)
- Grief-isolation of widower (Carl, Up)
- Found-family of misfits (Goonies, Mighty Ducks, Stranger Things)
- Indigenous kinship-with-land (Sweetgrass — backing #4)

**Holes:**
- **Post-divorce blended-family belonging** — the held-back is the disrupted prior tribe. Modern American family-genre.
- **Immigrant 2nd-gen between-tribes** — the held-back is *belonging to too many places imperfectly*. Specimens: Jhumpa Lahiri's *The Namesake*, Marjane Satrapi's *Persepolis*.
- **Online-community belonging** — the held-back is geographical/physical isolation; the tribe is digital. Cory Doctorow's *For the Win*, some fanfic / AO3 community theory.
- **Asexual community belonging** — distinct from individual ace identity; the community-formation itself.
- **Neurodivergent belonging** — the held-back is misfit with neurotypical norms; the tribe is the late-discovered autistic/ADHD community. Specimens: some Devon Price.
- **Diaspora across erasure** — the held-back is dispossession of land + language + memory; the tribe is reconstituted across loss. *Pachinko* (Lee), much Black-diasporic literature.

## mastery

**Present held-backs:**
- Total practical incompetence on desert island (Crusoe)
- Cosseted East-coast dog (Buck, Call of the Wild)
- Yogic-distraction-and-craving (Gita, four yogas)
- Buddhist mental untraining (Dhammapada, eightfold path)
- Stoic untrained ruling-principle (Aurelius's askesis)

**Holes:**
- **Late-life skill acquisition** — most mastery specimens are young-hero quests. The held-back of "I should have started this at 20" is its own dispossession. Specimens: parts of Helen Macdonald's *H is for Hawk*, much "late-bloomer" memoir.
- **Grief-skill / learning to mourn** — the held-back is being un-equipped for loss; the practice is mourning itself. C. S. Lewis's *A Grief Observed* runs partially; Joan Didion's *Magical Thinking*.
- **Digital-craft mastery** — programming-as-mastery, the held-back of beginner's incoherence; mass collaborative knowledge-work. Steven Levy's *Hackers*; some Ellen Ullman.
- **Mass-collaborative mastery** — the held-back of "I cannot do this alone"; the mastery built by community. Open-source histories; choral and orchestral memoirs.
- **Disability-acquired skill** — the held-back is bodily-altered-baseline; the mastery is the new equilibrium. Christine Miserandino, Alice Wong's *Disability Visibility* (editorship as mastery).

## redemption

**Present held-backs:**
- "Old sinner" greed-and-isolation (Scrooge)
- Treason against siblings under Deep Magic (Edmund)

**Holes:**
- **Collective-historical wrongs** — slavery, colonization, indigenous dispossession, the Holocaust. The held-back is the inherited or inflicted historical guilt. Cupel has *Souls of Black Folk* via virtue of defeat at collective scope (not redemption); the redemption-mode reading of collective wrong is under-represented.
- **Ecological wrongs** — the held-back is *what we did to the planet*; the atonement question. *The Overstory* (Powers) runs this partially; Wendell Berry essays.
- **Technological wrongs** — the engineer or designer who built the addictive system. *The Social Dilemma* (Orlowski) at non-fiction; *Anathem* (Stephenson) at the literary register.
- **Failed-parent redemption** — the held-back is "I damaged my child"; the atonement question. Some Marilynne Robinson (*Gilead*'s father-letter); some Toni Morrison.
- **Late-life conversion across irreversible wrongs** — Tolstoy's *Resurrection* (Nekhlyudov tracking the girl he wronged to Siberia). The held-back is "I cannot undo it" and the backing is sustained-witness rather than ritual atonement.

**Holes:**

## apotheosis

**Present held-backs:**
- Renaissance scholar with exhausted human disciplines (Faustus)
- Mortal human, poor and illiterate, dies under the mountain (Feyre)
- Chronically-injuring scribe-daughter (Violet)

**Holes:**
- **Maternal apotheosis** — the held-back is parenthood-as-loss-of-self; the ascension is the mother becoming-something-new. Very under-represented. Cusk's *A Life's Work* runs antagonist-mode; affirmative specimens absent.
- **Elder-power apotheosis** — most apotheosis specimens are young heroes. The held-back of "I was overlooked because old" + the ascension. Some Ursula K. Le Guin late work (Tehanu).

- **Posthuman / transhumanist apotheosis** — Egan's *Permutation City*, Banks's Minds. The held-back is biological-finitude.

## Methodological observations

1. **The held-back is where the most cultural-historical variation lives** — even more than backings. The same wish (e.g., liberation) gets stuck behind very different conditions across cultures and eras. The holes here are the most directly actionable for acquisition.
2. **Digital-era held-backs are under-represented across nearly every engine** — digital-attention-capture, algorithmic impunity, online toxicity, digital-elder-care, refugee-return-impossible-via-digital-borders. There's a coherent "digital condition" hole shape across the catalog.
3. **Climate-era held-backs are similarly under-represented** — climate-driven displacement, climate-grief, climate-adaptation safety, climate-archive legacy, climate-future-as-illegible. Another coherent hole shape.
4. **Mid-life and elder held-backs are under-represented in the romance / desire engines specifically** — most being-desired and apotheosis specimens are youth-arcs. This is a chronic catalog skew.
5. **The collective-political held-back keeps surfacing** — collective-historical wrongs (redemption), collective-political defeats (virtue of defeat — done), indigenous dispossession (multiple engines), collective apotheosis. The cupel catalog's individual-protagonist organizing assumption shows here as a structural gap.
