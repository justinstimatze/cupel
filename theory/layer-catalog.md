# Layer catalog — consumption-vs-content gap, per engine

**The move.** Cupel distinguishes two layers at which a work can run an engine:

- **Content layer** — the wish lives *inside* the story. Bella is desired; the reader is offered being-desired through Bella. (Most existing `works/` are content-layer reads.)
- **Consumption layer** — the work, as an object, *is* the engine vehicle. Owning / wearing / displaying / posting the work signals the wish. The content may be irrelevant; the object's social presence is the wish-payout.

Per-engine: which engines have consumption-layer specimens in the catalog, and where are the gaps?

---

## What consumption-layer reading surfaces

Most cupel cards omit the consumption-layer entirely or treat it as a footnote. The held-back of the consumption-layer reader is *being-the-wrong-person-in-the-eyes-of-the-tribe*; the engine pays out by *display*. The wish is identical (belonging, abundance, legacy) but the vehicle is the object itself, not the narrative.

Consumption-layer specimens (provisional, per engine):

### belonging
- **Prestige TV** as Sunday-night communal-viewing engine — *Succession*, *The Wire*, *White Lotus*. The watching IS the belonging; missed-it-this-week excludes you from the Monday office talk. Content runs belonging engines too (often crime/family) but the consumption-layer engine is the *communal participation*.
- **Star Wars / Marvel franchise badge-wearing** — the lightsaber prop, the Captain America shirt, the convention attendance. Belonging via shared object-reference.
- **The Brooklyn-indie / Sally-Rooney novel** as tote-bag literature — the carrying of the book is the signal.

### abundance
- **Luxury-brand catalogs** as the engine in pure form — Hermès look-books, Sotheby's auction catalogs. The catalogue *is* the savored-plenty (Ali Baba's signature move). The content is the cataloging; the consumption is the buying-or-aspiring.
- **Coffee-table books** qua coffee-table books — the *Taschen* art editions, the prestige food cookbook. Display-as-abundance.
- **Certain food-Instagram aesthetics** — the "abundance bowl," the curated pantry.

### legacy/transcendence
- **Collector editions** — Folio Society, Easton Press, the deluxe-hardcover heirloom market. The work *is* the inheritance-vehicle.
- **Museum-shop classics** — the marble bust of Marcus Aurelius on the desk. The object as legacy-signal.
- **University-bookstore canon shelves** — the "great books" assigned-reading display.

### apotheosis
- **Motivational posters / self-help shelves** — the Robbins library, the David Goggins audiobook subscription. The display = the ascending self.
- **CrossFit / Spartan-race medals** — the proof-of-elevation as wearable object.
- **"Library of greats" cover series** — the Modern Library, Penguin Classics; the bound set on the shelf as apotheosis-signal.

### repricing
- **NYT bestseller stickers, Booker / Pulitzer / Nobel banners** — the book as repricing-vehicle for the reader (the reader becomes the kind-of-person-who-reads-this).
- **Substack / *The Atlantic* / *NYRB* subscriptions** as identity-signal more than reading.

### order/legibility
- **The "smart-person" coffee-table books** — Pinker, Harari, Yuval; sometimes Diamond. Read partly, displayed entirely. The consumption-layer engine: I am the kind of person who understands.
- **The economist-newspaper subscription as identity** (much *Economist* circulation).

### being-desired
- **Hallmark / Tiffany / Cartier marketing** — the buyer purchases the *appearance* of being-desired (the diamond as desire-signifier).
- **Wedding-industrial-complex aesthetics** — the bridal magazine, the destination-wedding photo book.

### mastery
- **Tech-bro reading lists** — Naval, Marc Andreessen, "what I read in 2024" lists. The reading-as-mastery-signal.
- **Deliberate-practice nonfiction shelves** — Anders Ericsson, Cal Newport.

### security/safety
- **Doomsday-prepper material catalogs** — bug-out-bag inventories, Black Rifle Coffee.
- **NRA/gun-magazine subscriptions** as identity-signal.

### impunity
- **Libertarian-canon identity displays** — Ayn Rand boxed set on the shelf, "Don't Tread On Me" merch. The display = vicarious-impunity-claim.

### purity/contamination
- **Wellness-industry consumption** — Goop, "non-toxic" labeling, "clean eating" cookbooks. The buying = the purity-warding.
- **Organic / artisanal aesthetics** — at certain prices the products *are* the consumption-purity engine.

### redemption
- **12-step pamphlet displays** — the worn AA Big Book as identity.
- **Religious-bookstore evangelical-conversion shelf**.

### caretaking/being-needed
- **Parenting-book aesthetic displays** — Emily Oster, Janet Lansbury; visible on the shelf as identity-signal.
- **The "girl-mom" / "boy-mom" merch ecosystem**.

### virtue of defeat
- **Stoic-influencer merch** — "memento mori" coins, Ryan Holiday boxed sets. The Stoic-as-identity branding (often counterfeit per the Aurelius card's dual-use read).

### the double life
- **"Sigma-male" / red-pill / sovereign-individual canon displays** — the boxed set or audiobook subscription as the *secret-superior* badge. The owner reads as the I-am-not-like-them bearer; the display is the social claim.
- **Doomsday-prepper / off-grid identity aesthetics** at the *I-see-what-they-don't* register (overlaps with security/safety; the double-life leg fires when the surface signal is *secret superiority* rather than *protected-world surveillance*).

### recognition
- **Memoir-and-witness-literature shelves** as the *I-see-you* social claim — Vuong, Bechdel, Nelson, Baldwin on the visible shelf. The display = the witnessing-and-being-witnessed pose; the reader pre-loads the recognition pole the work runs at content-layer.
- **Book-club + queer-canon prestige circulation** — the recognition counterfeit at consumption-layer fires when the display *substitutes for* the slot-2 work the content-layer engine requires.

### wound
- **Sad-girl-novel / autofiction prestige displays** — Cusk, Ferrante, Knausgaard, Yanagihara on the shelf as the *I-bore-witness* badge. The consumption-layer counterfeit lands the audience as someone who *has been near* the wound (taste-as-witness) rather than someone with their own wound preserved. The wound-engine's structural non-transmission means the consumption-layer engine is almost always running a *neighbor-template* (often virtue-of-defeat or recognition-witnessing) — see [The interpreter and the engines](/cupel/theory/interpreter-and-engines/), section 2, on the neighbor-template substitution mechanism.

### homecoming/reunion
- **Nostalgia merch** — vintage band tees, "Class of [year]" reunion gear, family-heirloom display.
- **Heritage-DNA-test reports as social objects** (the 23andMe results shared on social media).

### unleashing
- **Motorcycle culture / Harley aesthetic** — the bike as license-display, the leather as engine-vehicle.
- **Extreme-sports gear sub-cultures** — the GoPro footage as discharge-display.

### liberation/autonomy
- **Van-life Instagram** — the build photos, the dispersed-camping content. The display = the breakout.
- **Certain anti-mainstream consumer identities** — "I don't own a TV" as identity; Mr. Money Mustache FI/RE culture.

## What this surfaces

1. **Every engine has consumption-layer specimens.** No engine is content-only. The consumption layer is structurally as wide as the content layer.
2. **Cupel currently under-tags consumption-layer reads.** Almost no dossier or review card flags the consumption-layer engine explicitly. The card format supports it (`layer: content | consumption`) but the convention has been content-only.
3. **The consumption-layer counterfeits are often easier to identify than the content counterfeits.** Goop's wellness-purity, gun-magazine surveillance-theater, Ayn Rand boxed-set impunity — these read as counterfeits at consumption-layer with less ambiguity than the same engines' content-layer counterfeits. Suggests the consumption-layer is *where the engine's commercial-counterfeit pipeline most visibly lives.*
4. **The consumption-layer and the recruitment-language hook are aligned.** Per [The claim](/cupel/theory/the-claim/), the sold ↔ self-administered spectrum, the v2 hook fires on recruitment-register language — the signature of "sold" counterfeits. Consumption-layer engines are mostly *sold* (someone makes money from your display); content-layer engines are often *self-administered* (the reader takes the wish privately). This may explain why cupel's runtime hook surfaces consumption-counterfeits more readily than content-ones.

## Holes

- **Per-engine consumption-layer specimen cards.** None currently exist as primary content-layer reads. A small batch (10-20 cards) explicitly tagged `layer: consumption` would close the structural gap.
- **The mixed-layer case.** Some works run both: *Atlas Shrugged* runs impunity at content-layer AND functions as consumption-layer libertarian-identity-signal. The catalog has no convention for tagging both.
- **The pure-consumption-layer counterfeit specimens.** Some Instagram / TikTok content runs no narrative at all and is *only* the consumption-layer engine running its counterfeit. The catalog doesn't currently include these as specimens; they may be the cleanest study cases for the engines' commercial-counterfeit pipelines.

## Status

The layer inventory is provisional — most consumption-layer specimens are named from cultural-canon familiarity rather than slot-tested. A proper layer-catalog deepening would slot-test specific objects (e.g., is the Tiffany diamond ad a slot-proven being-desired-at-consumption-layer specimen?) the same way [Backings inventory](/cupel/theory/backings/) slot-tested entries.

The consumption-layer gap is the most structurally important gap the inversion suite has surfaced that isn't already reflected in the runtime hook design — the runtime hook (`cmd/cupel/hook.go`) already targets sold counterfeits, which are mostly consumption-layer. The theory document and the runtime instrumentation are converging from opposite directions.
