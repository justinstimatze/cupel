# Blind spots — what cupel is not, and where it fails

The framework has known limits. Naming them is part of the discipline: a published taxonomy that won't admit its blind spots silently calibrates against the cases it can see and silently mis-files the cases it can't.

## Text-fed

The test reads fantasies carried in language — interiority, dialogue, the slow reveal of worth. It is blind to fantasies carried in the body and the spectacle. *John Wick*'s fantasy lives in the kinetics, not the words, so it will not slot from a transcript. "No engine found" can mean "an embodied fantasy the text can't show," not "no fantasy here."

## The consumption layer can't be slot-tested

Its wish lives in the reader's real-world behavior, not in the work. It stays an explicitly subjective section, gated by value-flow (does the badge substitute for the commitment, or enable it?), and kept out of the mechanical test. The per-engine consumption-layer inventory is in [layer-catalog.md](layer-catalog.md).

## Bluntness

Slot-fill produces Bechdel-style false negatives: a subtle case with no on-the-record line slips through. Accepted trade for checkability.

## Self-administered counterfeits

The runtime hook (`cmd/cupel/hook.go`) fires on recruitment-register language — the signature of the **sold** counterfeits (cult, MLM, PUA, etc.). The **self-administered** ones aren't pitched to anyone — they run in one head, emit no recruitment language, and so are structurally invisible to a recruitment-language detector. Part of the catalog is undetectable by this lens by construction. The structural argument is in [the-claim.md](the-claim.md) § "The sold ↔ self-administered spectrum."

## Demand/supply asymmetry

Some real demand-side drives have no isolable supply-side payoff in fiction. *Self-actualization* (the Personal-Legend wish) is the cleanest specimen: concretize "become who you're meant to be" and the payoff becomes another engine's (a great X = mastery; the treasure = abundance; seen-as-true-self = being-desired/repricing; ascend = apotheosis); keep it pure and it never pays out, only longs. So self-actualization reads as a real demand-side drive with no isolable supply-side engine — fiction gratifies it *only through* other engines. Logged in [falsification-log.md](falsification-log.md).
