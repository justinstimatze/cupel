# Bundle-shape catalog — engine combinations, recurring shapes, and the under-represented

**The move.** `cupel coverage`'s max-bundle leaderboard shows *which works run the most engines*. The bundle-shape catalog asks the inverted question: *which engine-combinations recur as recognizable shapes, and which shapes are structurally possible but under-represented?* Per-shape: present specimens, structural rationale, and specific candidate works that would fill the under-represented bundles.

A **bundle shape** is not just a co-occurrence; it's a recurring *combinatorial structure* where the engines reinforce each other in a culturally-recognized pattern. The romance bundle is a shape; "two engines happened to both appear in this work" is not.

---

## Well-represented shapes (the canonical bundles)

### Romance bundle
**Engines:** being-desired + abundance + protective-partner (often manifests as apotheosis or mastery in the partner).
**Specimens:** Twilight, ACOTAR, Fifty Shades, Fourth Wing.
**Structural rationale:** the wish to be wanted-as-you-are (being-desired) carries the wish to be materially-cared-for (abundance) and the wish for the desirer to be capable of protecting (apotheosis/mastery). The three engines lock together in the romantasy / contemporary romance / paranormal romance markets.

### Quest-fellowship bundle
**Engines:** belonging (fellowship) + mastery (the protagonist's skill-acquisition) + legacy/transcendence (the quest's stakes for the future).
**Specimens:** LOTR, Lightning Thief, Stranger Things, much epic fantasy.
**Structural rationale:** the band-of-companions makes the quest emotionally consequential (belonging), the quest requires the protagonist to grow into the task (mastery), and the stakes are framed as preventing a permanent loss to the world (legacy).

### Conversion bundle
**Engines:** redemption + belonging + new-self emerging.
**Specimens:** A Christmas Carol, Narnia.
**Structural rationale:** the wrong-self atones (redemption) and is welcomed back into the social order they had excluded themselves from (belonging) as a remade self.

### Hero's journey bundle (the Campbell template)
**Engines:** belonging-loss → mastery → apotheosis → homecoming/reunion.
**Specimens:** Star Wars (original trilogy), much Joseph-Campbell-pattern fiction.
**Structural rationale:** the hero leaves the ordinary world (belonging-loss), undergoes trials and trains (mastery), achieves ascended capability or power (apotheosis), and returns home transformed (homecoming/reunion). The classical template; recurs across cultures with backing variations.

### Underdog-sports bundle
**Engines:** repricing + belonging + mastery.
**Specimens:** The Mighty Ducks, Remember the Titans, Cool Runnings, much sports-fiction.
**Structural rationale:** the team is dismissed (repricing slot 1), forms into a real bond (belonging), trains into capability (mastery), and the win or moral-victory revalues them.

### Detective-puzzle bundle
**Engines:** order/legibility primarily, often solo.
**Specimens:** Holmes corpus, Gold-Bug, the Christie tradition.
**Structural rationale:** the engine runs strongly enough alone that the "bundle" is single-engine. Sometimes pairs with belonging (the Holmes-Watson partnership) or impunity (the criminal's freedom from the check the detective dispels), but the spine carries solo.

### Solarpunk gestalt bundle
**Engines:** liberation/autonomy (vocational redirection or sage-pole) + belonging at cultural-system scope + caretaking-as-reciprocity.
**Specimens:** Chambers (Monk & Robot), Le Guin (Always Coming Home), some Jemisin (Emergency Skin partial).
**Structural rationale:** the wish for a world configured so kindness works runs as combined freedom-from-extractive-frame + community-at-scope + reciprocal care. Cross-references the vocation-redirection candidate engine.

### Climate-political bundle
**Engines:** liberation/autonomy at solidarity scope + caretaking-of-the-future + virtue of defeat at collective-political scope.
**Specimens:** Robinson's *Ministry for the Future*; parts of Wallace-Wells's *Uninhabitable Earth*; partial in *Parable of the Sower*.
**Structural rationale:** the climate condition does not afford individual liberation, so the engine has to operate at collective scope, paired with care for unborn generations and the preemptive virtue-of-defeat stance toward already-arrived defeats.

## Under-represented shapes (the directed-acquisition queue)

### Care-witness bundle
**Engines:** caretaking/being-needed + virtue of defeat + legacy/transcendence.
**Structural rationale:** the carer who tends across hopeless circumstances and is remembered for it. The bond is to someone the carer cannot save; the virtue-of-defeat is the doing-anyway; the legacy is the witness preserved.
**Candidate specimens:**
- Tolstoy's "Master and Man" — the merchant Brekhunov dies sheltering Nikita his servant; runs caretaking + virtue of defeat in the death scene.
- Hospice and end-of-life memoir — Atul Gawande's *Being Mortal* (non-fiction); much hospice literature.
- Toni Morrison's *Beloved* runs this at extreme valence (Sethe's protection-care across an irreparable past).
- Marilynne Robinson's *Gilead* — the dying minister's letter to his son is care + legacy as one motion.

### Exile-and-return bundle
**Engines:** homecoming/reunion + repricing + redemption.
**Structural rationale:** the dispossessed who returns transformed; the home is changed and the returner is changed; the repricing is the returner's revaluation in the home that did not previously honor them.
**Candidate specimens:**
- Alex Haley's *Roots* — diaspora-return across colonial rupture; the catalog's canonical Black-American specimen for this shape.
- Yaa Gyasi's *Homegoing* — multi-generational repricing-and-return across the Ghana-America axis.
- *The Color Purple* (Walker) — Celie's return-to-self carries repricing and a partial redemption.
- *Pachinko* (Lee) — multi-generational migrant family; some return-via-grandchildren beats.

### Mystic-ascension bundle
**Engines:** liberation/autonomy at sage-pole + mastery (yogic/contemplative discipline) + apotheosis (mystical realization).
**Structural rationale:** the wisdom-tradition arc — the practitioner trains, attains mystical-realization, achieves the freedom that comes with it. Distinct from the romantasy apotheosis bundle by the *internality* of the ascent and the *spiritual* register.
**Candidate specimens:**
- The Bhagavad Gita — already in catalog; Krishna's discourse runs the bundle compactly.
- Hesse's *Siddhartha* — the canonical Western-literary specimen of the bundle.
- Maugham's *The Razor's Edge* — Larry Darrell's arc (already noted in candidate-vocation-redirection slot-test exclusions).
- Some Bhakti poetry (Mirabai, Kabir at the bhakti pole).
- The Tao Te Ching's sage-portrait chapters.

### Reckoning-without-redemption bundle
**Engines:** virtue of defeat + caretaking/being-needed + legacy/transcendence (often as personal-archive).
**Structural rationale:** the protagonist faces a life they cannot redeem; the meaning emerges from honest reckoning + the small bonds + what survives of the life into memory. Modern-litfic spine.
**Candidate specimens:**
- *Stoner* (Williams 1965) — the canonical specimen; runs virtue of defeat + a near-belonging-refusal + a small legacy.
- *Olive Kitteridge* (Strout 2008) — the cantankerous protagonist's reckoning over a community-life.
- *Lila* (Robinson 2014) — slot-tested 2026-05-29 against vocation-redirection candidate and excluded; now confirmed canonical specimen of the reckoning-without-redemption bundle running at the literary-fiction register with a partial-redemption fourth leg. Card: [Lila](/cupel/works/lila/).
- Late Tolstoy ("The Death of Ivan Ilyich") — the dying man's recognition of his life's emptiness, partially redeemed by his servant's care.

### Vocational-quietude bundle
**Engines:** caretaking/being-needed + belonging at small scale + mastery (vocational rather than heroic).
**Structural rationale:** the small life valued; the bond to a specific other; the practice as the vocation. Distinct from the conversion bundle by the absence of redemption (no wrong-self being atoned).
**Candidate specimens:**
- *Charlotte's Web* — already in catalog; carries the bundle clean.
- *The Little Prince* (Saint-Exupéry) — care + the rose + the fox.
- Annie Dillard's *Pilgrim at Tinker Creek* — vocational attention to a creek.
- Wendell Berry's Port William fiction — rural-vocational quietude as the wish.

### Worldbuilding-immersion bundle
**Engines:** belonging at *cultural-system scope* + abundance at *imaginative scope* (the world is full and varied) + legacy/transcendence (the world has history that outlasts characters).
**Structural rationale:** the wish to dwell in a *world* rather than to follow a *plot*. The engine-payout is to the reader who wants to inhabit the world long after the story ends. Heavy in epic fantasy and certain SF.
**Candidate specimens:**
- Tolkien's Middle-earth (LOTR runs this strongly).
- Le Guin's Earthsea — partial specimen; Le Guin AC at the anthropological pole.
- Pratchett's Discworld — comic-flavored specimen.
- Banks's Culture novels — SF specimen at the post-scarcity pole.

### Trauma-decoding bundle
**Engines:** order/legibility (trauma-as-illegible-experience) + virtue of defeat (the survivor's stance) + caretaking/being-needed (the witness or self-tending).
**Structural rationale:** the survivor's reconstruction of what happened; the meaning made from the unbearable; the care-for-the-younger-self or the others-similarly-wounded.
**Candidate specimens:**
- Carmen Maria Machado's *In the Dream House* — the canonical specimen.
- *Trauma and Recovery* (Judith Herman, 1992) — non-fiction; the framework text.
- Maggie Nelson's *The Argonauts* — partial.
- Many post-MeToo memoirs.

### Anti-trafficking-counterfeit bundle (the romance-trap counterfeit cluster)
**Engines:** being-desired + redemption — counterfeits running in parallel.
**Specimens (counterfeit-leaning):** Certain dark-romance subgenres ("the abuser can be saved by love"); the *Beauty and the Beast* counterfeit reading; some 80s romance.
**Structural rationale:** the being-desired wish is fulfilled by a desirer who *needs to be redeemed*; the heroine's love is the supposed atonement. Carries serious dual-use risk; the engine works only if the redemption is genuinely earned (Beauty and the Beast at its enabling pole; counterfeit when the abuse is romanticized as the path).

### Bearing-witness bundle (2026-05-29 addition, from the Maus card)
**Engines:** virtue-of-defeat + the-double-life-of-the-witness-once-removed + care-for-the-record (caretaking redirected from person to testimony).
**Specimens:** Art Spiegelman, *Maus* (1986/1991) — second-generation-Holocaust-testimony at graphic-novel register. Knausgaard's *My Struggle* (2009-2011) runs an adjacent radical-self-disclosure variant where the witness IS the participant rather than once-removed. Lawrence Wright's *Going Clear* (2013) and the NXIVM journalism corpus run the bundle at investigative-rendering register. Toni Morrison's *Beloved* (1987) runs the bundle at literary-fiction register where the historical-trauma rendering is the work.
**Structural rationale:** the bundle handles material the conventional engines cannot — historical-trauma at scope that cannot be redeemed (virtue-of-defeat), where the witness is positioned-after-the-event and must render-rather-than-fix (the-double-life-of-the-witness-once-removed), and where the engine's caretaking-mode operates at the *care-for-the-record* register rather than the dyadic-personal register. Methodologically distinct from the reckoning-without-redemption bundle (Stoner, Lila, etc.) by the *witness-once-removed* component — the protagonist is *not* the one who experienced the trauma but the one rendering it.
**Holes:** the bundle has been partially-named across multiple cupel cards but not formally inventoried until now. Worth a separate per-engine treatment in [Backings inventory](/cupel/theory/backings/) for virtue-of-defeat at the *historical-trauma-rendering* backing.

### Oath-binding-and-trauma-integration bundle (2026-05-29 addition, from the Sanderson Cosmere batch)
**Engines:** mastery + virtue-of-defeat + apotheosis, with mastery's slot-2 made operationally explicit via *vow-as-binding-cost* mechanism.
**Specimens:** Sanderson, *Words of Radiance* (2014) — Kaladin's Third-Ideal struggle, Shallan's identity-fracture integration; Sanderson, *Oathbringer* (2017) — Dalinar's Rift reckoning + Bondsmith vow. Both at epic-fantasy register; the bundle scales upward from the literary-fiction *reckoning-without-redemption bundle* (Stoner, Lila) with substantive depth preserved.
**Structural rationale:** the bundle uses Sanderson's hard-magic *vow-as-condition* mechanic to operationalize what literary fiction does internally — the protagonist's *spoken commitment* binds the magical-power, making mastery-counterfeit structurally impossible within the system (a Knight Radiant cannot fake the Ideals; the power would not respond). This is a literary argument *against* the cluster-catalog's mastery-counterfeit specimens (Tomassi's frame-control; Greene's 48 Laws; Robbins's NAC) at the metaphorical-fantasy-system scope.
**Related to** the reckoning-without-redemption bundle (literary fiction register) and to the *quest-fellowship bundle* (epic fantasy register); the oath-binding-bundle is the *operational* version where the slot-2 work has explicit magical-power consequences.

### Recruitment-cluster bundle (2026-05-29 addition)
**Engines (counterfeit pole):** apotheosis + impunity + order/legibility + at least one domain-specific counterfeit (mastery for self-help / seduction-mastery / startup-canon; purity + security for protected-world; belonging + redemption for cult). Plus a closed in-group lexicon.
**Specimens:** see four sibling cluster docs. Two slot-proven (protected-world, seduction-mastery), three wikipedia-grounded (self-help, startup-canon, cult), one narrative-mode (romance-trap, distinct shape).
**Structural rationale:** the three gravitational-center engines' counterfeits form a self-reinforcing recruitment-loop where the elevation justifies the exemption, the exemption is necessary because the framework's revealed order says conventional rules don't apply, and the revealed order is *evidenced* by the elevated buyers' success-stories. Mutual reinforcement via slot-2 deficits as evidence.
**Distinct from positive bundles**: the recruitment-cluster bundle is a *counterfeit* phenomenon by structural definition. The romance bundle (being-desired + abundance + protective-partner) and the romance-trap cluster (being-desired-counterfeit + redemption-counterfeit + apotheosis-counterfeit) are the same engines at opposite poles — the cluster-vs-bundle distinction may always be value-flow-grade rather than engine-identity.

### Founder-canon bundle (positive-pole; subset of startup-canon cluster)
**Engines (enabling pole specimens):** apotheosis + mastery + occasional legacy/transcendence (the company-as-lasting-thing).
**Specimens:** *Shoe Dog* (Knight 2016 — Nike origin memoir at the enabling pole), *Built to Last* (Collins & Porras 1994 — long-lived-company study), *The Innovator's Dilemma* (Christensen 1997 — the structural rather than heroic story).
**Structural rationale:** the same engine-set the startup-canon cluster runs at counterfeit can run at enabling when the founder's actual long-term work (building things that genuinely matter, learning, accepting feedback) is honored rather than bypassed. *Shoe Dog* is the cleanest contemporary specimen — Knight's account names the years of obscure tedious labor that the cluster-mode versions skip.
**Holes:** the *non-heroic-founder* shelf (engineering-craftsmanship memoirs, the founder-as-anonymous-team-member specimens, the family-business multi-generational specimens) is sparse in the catalog and in popular culture's available reading.

### Lifestyle-design bundle (counterfeit-leaning; subset of self-help cluster)
**Engines (mostly counterfeit pole, partially enabling):** liberation/autonomy at the *escape-the-corporate-cage* register + mastery + apotheosis (new-rich-as-class) + impunity (exempt-from-traditional-obligation).
**Specimens:** *4-Hour Workweek* (Ferriss 2007), FI/RE corpus (Mr. Money Mustache blog, *Your Money or Your Life* Robin & Dominguez 1992 / 2018), digital-nomad ecosystem (*Tropical MBA*, *Nomad List*).
**Structural rationale:** the lifestyle-design bundle was previously not named in the catalog; it surfaces during the self-help cluster slot-test as a four-leg shape running liberation/autonomy as the cluster's lead. Distinct from the rest of the self-help cluster (which runs apotheosis-led) — the lifestyle-design strand uses liberation as the *bait* for the rest of the cluster legs.
**Note**: *Your Money or Your Life* is closer to the **enabling** version of the bundle than 4HWW — Dominguez's framework was rooted in his own ascetic monastic-economic life and includes substantive financial-independence prescriptions. The cluster's enabling presence in this bundle is real.

### Stoic-influencer bundle (consumption-layer specimen of the self-help cluster)
**Engines:** mastery + apotheosis + virtue-of-defeat-trace + consumption-layer ritual.
**Specimens:** Ryan Holiday's *The Obstacle Is the Way* (2014), *Ego Is the Enemy* (2016), *The Daily Stoic* (2016) + the *Daily Stoic* podcast / annual reading / *memento-mori* merchandise.
**Structural rationale:** the Stoic-influencer bundle commodifies cupel's slot-proven Aurelius/Epictetus specimens (the source-tradition's substantive backing the wisdom-tradition entries already document) into a contemporary consumption-layer micro-purchase economy. **Engine-shift between source and commodification**: Aurelius's actual Stoic spine is liberation/autonomy (withdrawal-of-assent-to-judgment); Holiday's commercial-self-help spine is mastery (obstacle-conversion-as-trainable-skill). The bundle is partial-counterfeit at the framing — and engine-shifted from the source — but the underlying content has genuine value at the mastery-shape register. 

**Bundle entry revised 2026-06-05.** The original "virtue-of-defeat + apotheosis + mastery, with virtue-of-defeat-at-head methodological complication" framing was retracted after Holiday primary-text probe showed mastery as spine and virtue-of-defeat as trace-in-Part-III. See [The Obstacle Is the Way](/cupel/works/the-obstacle-is-the-way/).

## Structural meta-findings from the bundle-shape inventory

0. **Bundles split into positive-pole bundles and counterfeit clusters** (2026-05-29 addition). The romance bundle (enabling co-occurrence) and the romance-trap cluster (counterfeit co-occurrence at the same engines) are at the opposite ends of the same engine-set. The bundle-vs-cluster distinction may always reduce to *value-flow-grade*. Open methodological question for the engine-model docs: should the catalog formally split bundles by pole, or treat bundles-and-clusters as the same object at different grades?
1. **Romance and quest bundles dominate the catalog.** Both are well-explored; further additions in these shapes have diminishing returns relative to under-represented shapes.
2. **The reckoning-without-redemption and care-witness bundles are systematically under-represented.** Both center middle-of-life, low-glamor, ongoing-circumstance protagonists — exactly the held-back conditions named in [Held-back catalog](/cupel/theory/held-back-catalog/) as under-represented (mid-life, ongoing-care without resolution).
3. **The worldbuilding-immersion bundle is recognized but under-tagged in works/.** Tolkien is slot-proven, but the bundle-shape itself isn't named; future entries should explicitly tag the bundle when it runs.
4. **Counterfeit bundles parallel positive bundles structurally.** The counterfeit-cluster note already maps the protected-world / self-help / cult / romance-trap counterfeit bundles. Each positive bundle has a corresponding counterfeit shape; the bundle-shape catalog and the counterfeit-cluster catalog form complementary maps.
5. **Climate-era and digital-era bundles are emerging.** The climate-political bundle is a new specimen (Robinson). A *digital-condition bundle* — likely liberation/autonomy at attention-economy held-back + order/legibility at structural-inquiry + virtue of defeat in self-implicated mode (Tolentino's structure) — may be coalescing as a distinct shape. Worth watching across more specimens.
