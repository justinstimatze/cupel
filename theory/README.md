# theory/

The framework's working catalogs and structural findings — the *why* and *how* behind the per-work cards in `works/`. The README at the project root is the spec; the engine list lives there. This directory is what the spec stands on.

## Reference catalogs

These are the per-engine and per-axis inventories — the catalog's working substrate. New analytical moves go here first.

- **[glossary.md](glossary.md)** — canonical vocabulary. *bead*, *spine*, *also-runs*, *slot-proven* vs *reviewed*, *bearer-realizable*, *wish-valence guard*, etc.
- **[backings.md](backings.md)** — for each engine, the **named backing-registers** that fill slot 2. *Repricing*'s backings (cross-currency worth; correctable misvaluation), *mastery*'s (the regimen; the teacher), *liberation/autonomy*'s eleven backings (decisive break, jurisdictional-exit, contemplative-companioned, etc.). The hole-catalog move: what backings the catalog has named, and where the gaps are.
- **[held-back-catalog.md](held-back-catalog.md)** — for each engine, the **slot-1 conditions of being-stuck** the engine releases the protagonist from. The structural mirror to backings.md: what cages the catalog has named, and which kinds of stuck-ness aren't represented yet.
- **[layer-catalog.md](layer-catalog.md)** — for each engine, the **content-layer vs consumption-layer** split. Content-layer: the wish lives inside the story. Consumption-layer: the work is held as a tribal badge.
- **[bundle-shape-catalog.md](bundle-shape-catalog.md)** — recurring **engine combinations** as recognizable shapes. The romance bundle (being-desired + abundance + protective partner), the superhero bundle (the double life + apotheosis + belonging), the founder-novel cluster, etc. Under-represented bundles flagged as gaps.
- **[result-types.md](result-types.md)** — the **specimen-resolution taxonomy**. When a slot-test runs, what kinds of result are possible: clean fill, partial fill, fill-with-leak, split-result, solvent-not-engine, etc.
- **[cluster-catalog.md](cluster-catalog.md)** — the **recruitment-cluster catalog**. Each cluster is a recognizable formation (cult cluster, self-help cluster, startup-canon, TESCREAL, systemic-critique, climate-doom, etc.) running cluster-canonical engine machinery at scale. Per-cluster: pole-distribution (partial-refusal ↔ pure-counterfeit), canonical-text spectrum, outside-critique pole specimens.

## The counterfeit catalog and the falsification log

The catalog's two integrity-keeping documents — what's been confirmed and what's been excluded.

- **[counterfeit-catalog.md](counterfeit-catalog.md)** — for each engine, the **dark twin on a page**: the recruitment-pitch counterfeit, shown with primary-source quotes. The "same machine sold as a grift" register. Cult-cluster counterfeits (Dianetics, Heaven's Gate). Self-help counterfeits (Hill's *Think and Grow Rich*, Carnegie's *How to Win Friends*). Apotheosis's "unlock your god-mode" New-Thought ancestor (Trine). Each counterfeit is itself slot-tested.
- **[falsification-log.md](falsification-log.md)** — the **excluded** engines, works, and candidate moves. Election and vindication resolved as *solvents* (not engines). Transformation excluded as a *vehicle* (delivery device, not a wish). Specific work-by-work false-positive findings — *A Christmas Carol* fills unleashing's loose slots but is redemption. The integrity rule: log the negatives as carefully as the promotions.

## Framework findings

Structural results that hold across multiple slot-tests — methodology generalizations the catalog earned.

- **[foundational-moment-bistability.md](foundational-moment-bistability.md)** — mature recruitment-clusters split into parallel **partial-refusal ↔ pure-counterfeit** strands at their canonical-pair moments. Carnegie 1936 / Hill 1937 (self-help). Ries 2011 / Thiel 2014 (startup-canon). Andreessen 2023 / Yudkowsky+Soares 2025 (TESCREAL). The same canon-formation pressure that produces a canonical text produces its partial-refusal sibling.
- **[interpreter-and-engines.md](interpreter-and-engines.md)** — Michael Gazzaniga's **left-hemisphere interpreter** as the *demand-side neural substrate* the engines fit into. Why wish-fulfillment works at all: the interpreter constructs coherent self-stories from fragmentary inputs using templates; cupel's engines name the templates the interpreter reaches for.
- **[inversions.md](inversions.md)** — **analytical inversions** as a recurring research move. The hole-catalog move (backings inventory, held-back inventory) reads the catalog's *negative space* — what hasn't been named, where the engine-anatomy is mute. A methodology pattern that surfaces unmapped territory by inverting the question.
- **[texture-vs-engine.md](texture-vs-engine.md)** — the **demand-side test** as the discriminator between *engine* (the wish-about-life people consume the work for) and *texture* (the felt experience the work delivers — awe, dread, spectacle). Awe rides on order/legibility in *Jurassic Park*, on apotheosis in *2001*, on belonging in *Avatar* — texture is what the work feels like, not what people consume it for. Catches texture-for-engine slot-test slips; surfaced 2026-06-06 via the JP re-read.
