## The reading

**The bead.** To walk through a gate and find the deep-past rendered legible — extinct animals visitable, the unknowable cataloged and ticketed — and then to watch the legibility break in front of you, the very method that delivered the wonder turning loose what it promised to contain.

**Engines**
- **order/legibility-antagonist-mode** · content · spine · ~ — the world's deepest illegibility (extinct sixty-five million years gone, never-seen) is rendered legible by method: the amber-DNA-cloning pipeline, the animated cartoon explainer that walks the audience through the science, the automated tour-track and visitor-center that catalogs and contains the deep-past as a ticketed, mapped, walkable system. The slot-2 work the engine demands is *containment of the legibility* — control over what the method has summoned. Hammond's slot-2 was never paid ("spared no expense" is the *substitute*, money for the labor the engine required) — and the catastrophic failure of legibility is the film's load-bearing dramatization. The wish-fulfillment is rendered, then its counterfeit's bill comes due.
- **mastery** · content · also-runs · ~ — at the de-extinction-technology display register: the lab-tour, the cartoon walkthrough, the named-tech of Hammond's geneticists. Mastery operates as the methodological surface, not as a bearer-spine — there is no master-figure whose craft the film celebrates; the tech is black-box-delivered means.
- **abundance** · content · also-runs · ~ — at the catalogued-species-inventory register: the named-species roster, the visitor-center displays, the herd of brachiosaurs across an open plain. The plenty is *inventoried* rather than acquired or savored — closer to legibility's register than abundance proper, which is why it weakens from the earlier spine reading.

**The bundle.** Order/legibility supplies the wish (the deep-past made tractable and visitable); mastery supplies the methodological surface (the tech as visible delivery system); the abundance also-runs delivers the inventory-as-visible-catalog. All three run in antagonist-mode at the climax — the method fails, the inventory turns loose, the tech that mastered de-extinction cannot master containment. The wonder and the terror run on the same engine: legibility delivered, then legibility broken.

**Dual-use read.** Order/legibility's counterfeit is the *substitution of the substrate for the slot-2 work* — Hammond's "spared no expense" is precisely the counterfeit substituting money for the actual labor the engine required (containment, ethics, predictive understanding). The film half-runs the engine's enabling (the awe of seeing the dinosaurs IS the slot-2 work made visible for two acts) and half stages its counterfeit (the unpaid containment labor, the predictable disaster). Antagonist-mode locks in: the work is the engine's named refusal.

**Texture vs engine.** The film's load-bearing supply-side texture is *awe* — the brachiosaur reveal, the swelling music, the faces tilting up. Awe is ride-along texture, not engine: it's how the order-engine rendering-the-deep-past-legible *feels*, not what it *is*. The earlier "abundance-as-wonder" spine read was confusing texture for engine — the catalogued-plenty was sitting in for the awe-experience, which was sitting in for the actual wish (the deep-past made visible-and-legible). The corrected read: order/legibility-antagonist-mode at the spine, awe as the texture, abundance demoted to inventory-register also-runs.

**Verdict.** The blockbuster specimen of order/legibility-antagonist-mode at the deep-past-rendered-visitable register, with awe-texture and abundance-inventory riding along.
