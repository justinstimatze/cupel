## The reading

**The bead.** An innocent child is defiled from outside by something monstrous, and two priests pay with their lives to drive it out and hand her back clean.

**Engines**
- **purity/contamination** · content · spine · ~ — the defiled-victim shape at its starkest: Regan, a child who did nothing to earn it, is contaminated from without (the body desecrated, the voice and flesh no longer hers), and a costly ritual cleansing — the Roman Ritual, paid for with both priests' lives — expels the contaminant and restores her; she wakes clean, with no memory of the defilement. The bright pole, purity actually restored.
- **redemption** · content · also-runs · ~ — Father Karras's crisis of faith resolves in a sacrificial coda ("Take me!"): he pulls the demon into himself and throws himself out the window. It is the cleansing's *cost*, not the spine.
- **caretaking/being-needed** · content · also-runs · ~ — Chris MacNeil's desperate maternal fight — the mother who exhausts medicine and then the church — drives the search for the cure.

**The bundle.** The purification bundle: a non-moral defilement imposed on an innocent (purity) lifted by a sacrificial cleansing (redemption's cost) undertaken at a mother's behest (caretaking).

**Dual-use read.** Purity's counterfeit is the purity spiral / scapegoating — declare a contaminant irreversible, then purge it (the Madison Grant register, [counterfeit-catalog](/cupel/theory/counterfeit-catalog/)). The Exorcist sits firmly on the enabling side: the contaminant is real, the cleansing is real and costly, and the defilement is **non-moral** — Regan is a victim, not a sinner, which is the guard that separates purity from redemption. The hazard register is faint here; the film is the engine's clean home, the demonic-possession genre's reason to exist.

**Verdict.** The bright-pole companion to *Dracula*: purity/contamination delivered positively, the contaminant confronted and expelled at mortal cost and the stainless self handed back — exactly the wish possession-horror is built to service.
