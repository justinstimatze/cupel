## The reading

**The bead.** To be made safe from a lethal threat — behind a real wall, built and held at real cost, the harm rendered helpless and you out of its reach.

**Engines**
- **security/safety** · content · spine · ✓ — the *fortified-refuge* shape: a lethal threat bears down (Silver's mutineers; "the cry of a man at the point of death"), a real defense is held at the cost of a life (Redruth, "carried, groaning and bleeding, into the log-house"), and behind the wall "too strong to pull down without time and labour" the besieged are safe ("they stood quiet in shelter and shot the others like partridges").

**Dual-use read.** Security's counterfeit is **the protection racket** — *you are not safe, the threat is everywhere, and only we can protect you — so submit.* The documented ancestor is Hobbes' *Leviathan* (#3207): the threat inflated to the state-of-nature maximum ("such a warre, as is of every man, against every man"; life "nasty, brutish, and short") and safety sold by total submission to "a common Power to keep them all in awe." The wish-valence guard is the difference: Stevenson's safety is bought with a *real* defense against a *real* threat at real cost (the engine **enables** — the wall is genuinely strong, the watch genuinely kept, the price paid in Redruth's life), where the counterfeit grants the protected feeling without any defense you raise, manufacturing or inflating the threat to compel the surrender (**substitutes**). The modern form is the strongman / security-state / "only we can keep you safe." Value-flow call is subjective, per the README — Treasure Island sits on the enabling side (the defense is dramatized and costly), but it is a clean fictional statement of the wish the protection racket hijacks.

**Consumption.** Stockpile against the coming threat to *feel* safe — the prepper's gear or the strongman's promise standing in for any real defense against a real danger.

**Verdict.** Security/safety's primary specimen and the **second prevention-pole engine**: an adventure novel's siege runs the engine because the engine *is* a dread (of harm) and its relief (the defense that holds), not an approach to a wished identity. ## The evidence

The clean specimen of the engine's **fortified-refuge** shape: a lethal threat bears down, a real defense is built and held at cost, and the besieged are made safe. Crucially there is **no contamination/defilement register anywhere** — the danger is pure violent harm and the remedy is pure physical defense — which is what isolates security/safety from the other prevention-pole engine, purity.

### Slot 1 — The threat (dread of violent harm)

The danger is bodily, lethal, and external: Silver's mutineers, intent on murder. The dread is made audible the moment the party splits:

> "there came ringing over the island the cry of a man at the point of death" (l. 3438)

The threat is an army-scale assault on the refuge — the besiegers numerous enough that the garrison reckon their odds against "a regiment."

### Slot 2 — The defense, at cost

Safety is bought with real labour and real blood. The stockade is a genuine work, not a token — its strength is *defined* by the cost it would take to breach:

> "without door or opening, too strong to pull down without time and labour" (l. 3428)

And reaching and holding it costs a life — old Redruth, shot getting over the wall:

> "the poor old gamekeeper hoisted over the stockade and carried, groaning and bleeding, into the log-house" (ll. 3788–3789)

> "now, when we had laid him down in the log-house to die" (l. 3793)

### Slot 3 — Safety achieved

Behind the real wall, the lethal threat is rendered helpless; the besieged are made safe:

> "they stood quiet in shelter and shot the others like partridges" (ll. 3430–3431)

> "short of a complete surprise, they might have held the place against a regiment" (ll. 3431–3432)

### Guard / distinctness — security, not purity, not mastery

The wish-valence guard holds: safety is **earned through a real defense against a real threat**, not conjured. The stockade works *because* it is genuinely strong and well-watched ("All they wanted was a good watch and food"), and the cost is paid on the page (Redruth's life, the lost second boatload of stores).

Two adjacencies are checked and cleared:

- **Not purity.** The threat carries no defilement, contamination, or uncleanness valence — it is murder, not pollution; the defense is a log wall, not a cleansing. This is the isolation the bundling question wanted: security/safety separates from purity on the **first** specimen, where purity had to be hunted across three texts before the ritual register isolated it.
- **Not mastery.** The gratification is *being out of reach of the harm*, not *earned capability through a regimen*. The garrison defends; it does not train or improve. (Adventure/valor is the genre frame around it, but the fortified-refuge beat itself is pure security.)

### Result

All three slots fill against a purely physical, lethal threat. Tagged **security/safety** — the fortified-refuge shape, the second confirmed **prevention-pole** engine (undo a dreaded harm, vs the approach-pole engines' reach for a wished identity).
