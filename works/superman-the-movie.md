## The reading

**The bead.** A being of godlike power chooses to live among us as a meek nobody, and the wish it pays is the daydream of being secretly, limitlessly more than you appear.

**Engines.** Apotheosis and impunity are two structurally distinct engines co-running at route-3 (fictional worldbuilding-bearer-realizable) — the superhero/cape register's signature stack.
- **apotheosis** · content · spine · ~ — the godlike-form / ascended-being shape: Superman as the Kryptonian who has ascended beyond typical-human form. The viewer's payout includes the imagined-experience of inhabiting the ascended-body. Spine genuinely contested with impunity and the double life — Donner's film is as much about Clark as about the cape, and as much about *acts-without-typical-consequence* as about *ascended-form*.
- **impunity** · content · spine · ~ — at route-3 sub-variant A (invulnerability) plus sub-variant C (consequence-reversibility, in the film's reverse-time-to-save-Lois ending). *Held back by typical-human consequence (bullets, mortality, accountability); released by Kryptonian biology (sub-variant A) plus the act-defying physical-law capability (sub-variant C — reversing Earth's rotation to undo Lois's death is the cleanest possible consequence-reversibility-impunity beat ever filmed)*; payout = acts-without-typical-consequence, both in-the-moment and retroactively-edited. The "godlike body itself" wish the original review named for apotheosis includes the impunity-shape that the catalog now distinguishes.
- **the double life** · content · also-runs · ~ — Clark Kent the bumbling reporter over Superman the god; the glasses, the costume-change gag, Lois's blindness to the man in front of her. The secret extraordinary self is the film's running engine.
- **legacy/transcendence** · content · also-runs · ~ — Jor-El's mission and inheritance ("the son becomes the father and the father the son"; "they can be a great people… they only lack the light to show the way"), the El name carried to Earth.
- **belonging** · content · also-runs · ~ — Smallville and the Kents; the orphan of a dead world finding a home and a people to protect.

**The bundle (revised — five engines, not four).** The Superman stack *looks* like six engines, the previous review reduced to four by collapsing two pairs (being-desired into double-life, repricing into double-life). The 2026-06-05 impunity retag *adds* one back: apotheosis and impunity were collapsed in the original tagging and now split. The corrected count is **five engines: apotheosis + impunity + the double life + legacy + belonging**, all clearing the wish-valence guard. This breaks the 2-hour-runtime "4-plateau" finding documented at-148 — a fictional film with worldbuilding-real impunity-shape can carry five engines because the impunity and apotheosis are *co-located in the same bearer* and the viewer inhabits both via the same POV without crowding-out. **Methodological finding:** the 4-plateau holds under disciplined tagging *when impunity isn't co-located with apotheosis in the same bearer*; the Superman case shows that double-loading the same bearer with two engines whose payouts share the same imagined-experience (impunity + apotheosis = the godlike-body wish) lets the stack break 4 in the runtime-budget. **Being-desired stays cut** (Lois desires Superman, not Clark — guard fails). **Repricing stays cut** (the receipt is never delivered as Clark — the secret is kept; folds into double life).

**Discriminator finding.** Superman is the cleanest single-case specimen of route-3 sub-variants A + C running co-located in one bearer. The film's reverse-time-to-save-Lois ending is the catalog's purest sub-variant-C beat in any film. The impunity transmits to the viewer via POV-inhabitation; no operational tactics are required.

**Dual-use read.** Apotheosis's counterfeit is the "unlock your god-mode / realize your divinity" grift (Trine's New Thought, [counterfeit-catalog](/cupel/theory/counterfeit-catalog/)); the double life's is the "you are secretly extraordinary, the sheeple can't see it" flattery. Superman sits on the enabling side — the power serves a chosen restraint and an ideal — but it is the cleanest fictional statement of both wishes the counterfeits hijack.

**Verdict.** The maximal-stack test for the catalog: the ur-superhero, naively "all the engines," lands at a disciplined **four** — because the wish-valence guards dissolve being-desired and repricing into the double life. Evidence that the apparent 5–6-engine bundle is facet-double-counting, and that four is holding as the real ceiling.
