## The reading

**The bead.** A flat-earth-doctrine-mandatory theocratic company-town in Lake County, Illinois (1901 founded by John Alexander Dowie, 1906–1942 controlled by Wilbur Glenn Voliva) — the catalog's clearest historical specimen of flat-earth-doctrine running as the order/legibility-leg of a cult cluster rather than as the contemporary protected-world cluster's distributed-no-leader register. Voliva took control after a 1906 congregational revolt against Dowie; from 1914 he made flat-earth advocacy a load-bearing church doctrine and mandatory in the church schools; the cult collapsed in stages (1937 Shiloh Tabernacle fire, personal bankruptcy, city governance reverted to "independents," a globe sticker imposed on his car) before his October 1942 death sealed dissolution.

## The evidence

The catalog's flat-earth ∩ cult-cluster historical specimen, graduated from `~ reviewed` to `✓ slot-proven` via Garwood 2008, Cook 1996, and Schadewald (posthumous *Plane Truth* Ch. 8). Voliva's prophet-gift mastery runs at a *positional-doctrinal* sub-mode distinct from the *charismatic-revelatory* sub-mode of Jones / Asahara / Applewhite, confirmed by Garwood's character-distinction from Dowie and by Cook's independent assessment.

### Mastery (positional-doctrinal prophet-gift)

Voliva names the slot-1 in *Leaves of Healing*, May 10 1930:

> "In the Christian Catholic Apostolic Church in Zion we accept the Bible as the Inspired Word of God. We believe that the Bible, in the original languages it was written, was verbally inspired – that is, that not only the thoughts were inspired but also the words. We accept all that the Bible has to say regarding the earth, the sun, the moon, and the stars, and **we do not propose to surrender the Word of God to infidel astronomers**." (Voliva, *Leaves of Healing*, 10 May 1930; quoted Garwood l. 1643)

Voliva's authority register is positional-doctrinal — divinely-appointed-overseer enforcing the divine doctrine, with the doctrine itself rather than personal charisma load-bearing:

> "A principal problem was the loss of divine authority. Dowie, for all his alleged lapses, was widely viewed as God's chosen leader bestowed with visible healing gifts, and many found it difficult to place a similar level of faith in his bombastic successor. Undoubtedly Voliva's gifts were of a more worldly order. A dictatorial preacher, who appeared to revel in conflict and dispute, when he warned his congregation 'the war is on red hot' the threat was truly meant." (Garwood l. 1696)

> "Upon Dowie's passing, a new theocrat, Wilbur Glenn Voliva, became leader. Lacking Dowie's charisma, but possessing keen administrative skills, Voliva restored the utopia's economic base…" (Cook l. 358)

The enforcement apparatus:

> "the Zion Guard, Voliva's special police force, who carried Bibles rather than guns in their holsters. At one time the guard numbered eight hundred men, employed to control law-breaking and moral lapses." (Garwood l. 1713)

> "Voliva instructed his assistant, Anton Darms, to search the scriptures for evidence of the earth's shape, and although he failed to find explicit mention of flatness, he discovered many passages referring to corners or ends. Consequently, words such as 'ball' were edited from Zion hymn books, while Voliva later published Darm's findings, William Carpenter's *One Hundred Proofs that the Earth is not a Globe*, and special issues of *Leaves of Healing* to promulgate his unconventional world-view." (Garwood l. 1730)

Lifestyle-laws: no liquor, tobacco, profanity, dancing, cinemas, theatres, congregations of more than two, chewing gum, driving over 5 mph; mandatory bathing 50ft apart; women's clothing-laws. Distinct from trainable-craft (no methodology members acquire) and from charismatic-revelatory prophet-gift (no personal supernatural display).

### Apotheosis (theocratic-elect-city)

Dowie's founding framing carried through:

> "Dowie saw the city as the ultimate gesture of his belief, more meaningful than any sermon, for it was established as a true theocracy, a city ruled by God. He saw to it that Zion was wholly owned and controlled by the Christian Catholic Apostolic Church, a place where the divine would influence every aspect of family, commercial, educational, ecclesiastical and political life… From its inception, Zion was designed to be the model Christian colony, a city in which God's people could escape the poverty, disease and sinful ways of the outside world." (Garwood l. 1672)

Real estate sold at market rates but conveyed under 1,100-year leases subject to termination at the General Overseer's whim. The city is the slot-2; ascent is moving inside the boundary. *Leaves of Healing* called Zion "a refuge for the oppressed of God's people" (Garwood l. 1672). Apotheosis delivered as collective theocratic-elect-status rather than individual personal ascent.

### Order/legibility (physical-cosmology-empirically-defended)

Voliva names modern astronomy, evolution, and higher criticism as the "trinity of evils":

> "Modern Astronomy, Evolution, and Higher Criticism are a trinity of evils… doctrines of seducing demons." (Voliva, 1930 publication, quoted Schadewald Chapter 8)

The phrasing recasts mainstream science not as merely-wrong but as spiritually-malign — characteristic of the cult-cluster's totalizing-frame intensification of order/legibility. Schadewald gives the chronological onset precisely: first flat-earth sermon August 16, 1914; explicit flat-earth doctrine December 26, 1915. The December 1915 sermon delivers the slot-1+slot-2 nexus:

> "I believe this earth is a stationary plane; that it rests upon water… It is a lot of silly rot, born in the egotistical brains of infidels." (Voliva sermon, December 26, 1915, quoted Schadewald Chapter 8)

The defense apparatus:

> "The highlight [of *Leaves of Healing*] was a photograph of a twelve-mile stretch of the shoreline of Lake Winnebago, Wisconsin, taken with an eight-by-ten-inch Eastman view camera with its lens exactly three feet above the water. 'With a good pair of binoculars,' the caption read, 'one can see small objects on the opposite shore; anyone can go to Oshkosh and see this sight for themselves any clear day. The scientific value of this picture is enormous,' the caption continued, for it was incontrovertible proof of the flatness of the lake and the earth itself." (Garwood l. 1730)

> "[Voliva] argued that the earth was as flat as 'a saucer, a pancake or a stove lid', with the North Pole at its centre and the South Pole distributed around its circumference as an immense barrier of ice. This barrier prevented ships from sailing over the edge of the disc, Voliva asserted, and he was so convinced of its existence that at one time he planned to embark on a voyage around the sixty-fifth meridian, just above the Antarctic Circle and near the supposed rim, to prove his theory incontestably." (Garwood l. 1732)

Plus the WCBD radio (~1922; audible to Central America), the Nobile / Byrd public bets, the *Handbook and Guide to Hell*, and the standing $5,000 challenge (Schadewald: "$5,000 to anyone who could prove to him that the earth is a globe, and nobody ever collected"). The challenge functions as in-group rhetorical authority rather than as actual epistemological accountability — no claimant ever satisfied Voliva's criteria, by design.

The flat-earth canon-lineage Voliva drew on (per Schadewald): Samuel Rowbotham (*Zetetic Astronomy*, 1865 — the canon's foundational PD text); William Carpenter (*One Hundred Proofs that the Earth is not a Globe*); Alexander Gleason (flat-earth maps); Lady Blount (*Earth not a Globe Review*); Thomas Winship (*Zetetic Cosmogony*); the Boston flat-earthers Charles Morse, John Abizaid, Charles DeFord. The sub-register predates Voliva's cluster-use by 50 years — single-engine canon-founding (Rowbotham 1865), inherited by cult-mode (Voliva 1914-1937) and later by distributed protected-world-mode. Cluster-mode-portable from canon-founding.

### Impunity (theocratic enforcement)

The Zionite submits to lifestyle restrictions, donates worldly goods, observes Sundays where "pleasure pursuits of any kind – including whistling and reading newspapers – were outlawed" (Garwood l. 1713).

The enforcement apparatus is exempt from ordinary civic accountability:

> "Vote-rigging and church-burning, riots and fights: independent Zionites were allegedly subjected to ongoing persecution from Voliva and members of his Theocratic Party over the years that followed. Denounced as dirty dogs and worse, their shopping street was nicknamed 'Rat Row', their children were forced to attend different schools from those that were church-affiliated and were even banned from playing in Zion's parks, which Voliva personally owned." (Garwood l. 1694)

> "It was rumoured that spying on neighbours was encouraged, and that any citizen of Zion who forsook the Shiloh Tabernacle for an alternative risked being labelled a traitor and excommunicated by the Church. Social exclusion, if not exile, was likely, and since Voliva owned or controlled many businesses, errant worshippers also risked their livelihoods and homes." (Garwood l. 1698)

Crucially less-violent than Aum's *poa* doctrine of altruistic murder, but at the same structural register: in-group enforcement insulated from ordinary-civic accountability.

Voliva's personal exemption: amassed wealth + deathbed confession of fund-misappropriation; 1937 personal bankruptcy with $1M+ debts; Schadewald documents specifically that Voliva "established a $75,000 fund for personal residences while [the] city was bankrupt." The founder-takes-while-followers-give register at operational specificity.

### The decay-during-life timeline

Garwood's chronicle (ll. 1831-1833):

> "Although Voliva's catastrophic predictions for 1935 had been inaccurate, **his personal empire met its end that year. Worn down by economic depression, failed promises and a repressive regime, the people of Zion deserted him and his Theocratic Party in the city elections and his twenty-nine-year rule was finally over.** He was reduced to honorary president of Zion's bankrupt industries and became one religious leader among many in the city. The most positive development was the first presentation of Zion's famous passion play in the Shiloh Tabernacle, fully supported by Voliva as an inspiring and effective means of presenting the gospel to the public. By the end of the 1936 season, 60,000 people had seen the story of Jesus of Nazareth, although the following year's production could not claim similar success. While the run commenced with good attendances, the Tabernacle and WCBD were burned down in early April [1937] by a teenage arsonist with a grudge against Voliva. The fire and the general lack of funds in the troubled city led to the closure of the briefly reopened parochial schools and an end to the flat-earth syllabus." (Garwood l. 1831)

> "Later that year [1937], Voliva was declared bankrupt with debts of more than a million dollars, and **in April 1938 the new authorities selected the image of a globe as the design for Zion's compulsory vehicle-tax sticker.** The Oshkosh Northwestern revelled in the fact that Voliva would be forced to display the picture of a spherical earth on the windshield of his car and, moreover, **that it had been created by a former pupil of his church schools, which were closed permanently in 1939.**" (Garwood l. 1833)

Operational dissolution:
- **1935** — lost city elections; "twenty-nine-year rule finally over"; reduced to honorary president
- **1936** — passion-play season (60,000 attendees), the last successful Voliva-affiliated cultural event
- **April 1937** — teenage arsonist burns Shiloh Tabernacle and WCBD radio station; flat-earth school syllabus ends
- **Later 1937** — Voliva declared personally bankrupt with $1M+ debts
- **April 1938** — globe vehicle-sticker imposed (created by a former pupil of his church schools)
- **1939** — church schools closed permanently
- **October 1942** — Voliva dies; church "all but dissolved"; small remnant reorganized under Michael Mintern as Christ Community Church without flat-earth doctrine

The cluster's substantive operational existence ends 1935 (electoral defeat); the prophet-gift register dissolves 1935-1939; the 1942 death seals what is already a multi-year unraveling. The catalog's cleanest documented decay-during-life timeline — seven years of operational dissolution before founder-death.

The refined mastery-leg two-register prediction holds across both prophet-gift sub-modes — Voliva at the positional-doctrinal sub-mode (no personal supernatural display; authority via divinely-appointed-overseership) and Jones / Asahara / Applewhite at the charismatic-revelatory sub-mode (personal supernatural display load-bearing). The mechanism is not specific to charismatic-revelation but to the prophet-gift register's general non-transferability.
