(l. 485)

The Vanguard register operationalized through the *tribute* ritual:

> "Why were the leaders instructing us to refer to NXIVM's founder, Keith Raniere, by the name Vanguard? Why were we paying what they called 'tribute' to him, like prayers recited as a group, when he wasn't even in the room?" (l. 552)

> "We left on the final day in the manner Nancy had instructed us, paying tribute to NXIVM's founder, Keith, by bowing while saying, 'Thank you, Vanguard.'" (l. 664)

The recruitment frame's *most-noble-man-I've-ever-met* register at Vanguard Week:

> "One called Keith 'the most noble man I've ever met,' and I couldn't decide whether I found all the unabashed tributes to Keith nauseating or inspiring." (l. 914)
