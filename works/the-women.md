## The reading

**The bead.** The reader is paid the wish that real, costly work the world refused to count will finally be seen and valued — that a denied service is owed, and gets, its true worth.

**Engines**
- **repricing** · content · spine · ~ — Frankie's combat-nursing in Vietnam is heroic, demanding labor, yet she comes home to a country that insists "there were no women in Vietnam" — denied veteran standing, turned away, told her contribution does not count. The hold is not inability but others' wrong valuation; the whole arc bends toward the recognition that re-prices her service as real and worthy. (In-copyright — no verbatim quote.)

**The bundle.** Single-spine: undeniable work, systematically undercounted, released by being recognized at last.

**Dual-use read.** Repricing's counterfeit is the grievance loop — clinging to the wrong valuation as identity, the unseen-ness worn rather than overturned. The book does not run it; it is the bright pole, the denial is named only so the recognition can land.

**Verdict.** Fits cleanly. Repricing spine; belonging (a place among the women who served) hovers as a faint also-run but stays subordinate to the recognition wish.
