## The evidence

The systemic-critique cluster's intra-critique bridge canonical specimen. Coates sits inside the cluster's political project but refuses the cluster's own myth-temptations from within — a structurally distinct bridge type that surfaces the intra-critique-bridge subtype as a new structural finding.

### Engine 1 — Order/legibility (writer's-responsibility-to-the-particular)

The book's analytical move: a writer's responsibility to the truth-of-the-particular held *above* the utility-of-the-political-myth. The leg fills at the **partial-refusal pole** because the framework holds the writer's craft as the substantive analytical responsibility.

### Engine 2 — Virtue of defeat (refusal-of-vindicationist-myth from inside the tradition)

**The bridge-position-signature key passage — refusing his own cluster's myth-making from inside:**

> "I was born into what the historian St. Clair Drake calls the 'vindicationist tradition,' that is, to Black people who sought to reclaim the very history weaponized against them and turn it back against their tormentors. If a 'Black Egypt' was what the Niggerologists feared, then we would insist on its truth and take it to its logical conclusion: We were born not to be slaves but to be royalty. That explains our veneration of Black pharaohs and African kingdoms. The point was to tell a different story than the one imposed on us—an understandable response, but one that I've never made peace with" (Ch. on Senegal)

The moral-elevation move: held now by the bearer's *refusal of the vindicationist mythology* he was raised in. Bearer-realizable, conditional on the slot-2 discipline (the ongoing refusal of myth-making temptation), the intra-critique-bridge subtype's distinguishing signature.

### Engine 3 — Belonging (liberation-without-myth)

**The bridge-position-signature key passage — refusing the in-group identity-formation move of myth-creation:**

> "I am trying to urge you toward something new—not simply against their myths of conquest, but against the urge to craft your own.… This is difficult, if only because so much of our myth-making was done in service of liberation, in doing whatever we could to dig our way out of the pit into which we were cast. And above us stand the very people who did the casting, jeering, tossing soil into our eyes and yelling down at us, 'You're doing it wrong.' But we are not them, and the standards of enslavers, colonizers, and villains simply will not do. We require another standard—one that sees the sharpening of our writing as the sharpening of our quality of light" (Ch. on Senegal close)

The in-group identity offered is *liberation-without-myth* — the political project preserved, the myth-making temptation refused.

### Cross-leg summary — the intra-critique-bridge subtype signature

The counterfeit-operator-location rule fires inside the text — Coates names the myth-making temptation (his own cluster's pure-counterfeit-pole-leaning move) as the temptation he refuses. **Fourth independent confirmation of the rule** across clusters (after Spade in the systemic-critique cluster, Clear in self-help, Fern + Cooley in polyamory canon). The intra-critique-bridge subtype is structurally distinct from Kendi's per-leg-pole-mixing bridge: Coates refuses the cluster's pure-counterfeit-pole moves *from inside the cluster*, where Kendi mixes poles *per-leg*.
