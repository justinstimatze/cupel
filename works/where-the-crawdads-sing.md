## The reading

**The bead.** The abandoned, unseen girl is finally wanted — chosen by a man, by a town, by the reader who has been aching for her to be.

**Engines**
- **being-desired** · content · spine · ~ — The whole arc is Kya held back by being unchosen: deserted by mother, father, brother, then by the boy who loved her; branded the feral "Marsh Girl" no one will claim. The payout the reader is fed is her being wanted — Tate's return, the recognition of her worth, the longing-to-be-chosen answered. Modern/in-copyright: structural read, no quote.

**The bundle.** A clean single-engine book — the loneliness-to-being-wanted line carries everything.

**Dual-use read.** being-desired's counterfeit is the grievance fantasy where the unchosen punishes those who passed her over; the novel flirts with it in the verdict-twist but keeps its weight on the bright pole of being loved, not on revenge.

**Verdict.** A pure being-desired engine — the unseen made wanted.
