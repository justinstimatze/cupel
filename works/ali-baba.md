## The reading

**The bead.** To be lifted out of want into a mass of described, catalogued plenty — the savored wealth itself, named and heaped, felt against the poverty it replaces.

**Dual-use read.** Abundance's counterfeit is **the luxe life sold minus the means** — *you too can have all this; the abundance is one mindset away; no capital, no labor, no luck required.* The documented ancestor is Wallace D. Wattles' *The Science of Getting Rich* (#59844), the New Thought root of the "abundance mindset": it keeps the slot-3 payoff ("The desire for riches is really the desire for a richer, fuller, and more abundant life") while denying the slot-2 means ("No one is prevented from getting rich by lack of capital"; "You may be the poorest man on the continent, and be deeply in debt; you may have neither friends, influence, nor resources; but if you begin to do things in this Way, you must infallibly begin to get rich"). It is distinct from apotheosis's Trine even though both are New Thought: Wattles' payoff is relentlessly *material* (money, property, "money enough to buy all he wants"), not divinity or power. (Corroborating: Veblen's *The Theory of the Leisure Class* (#833) names the bait mechanism — "Conspicuous consumption of valuable goods is a means of reputability" — the displayed plenty that drives pecuniary emulation.) The wish-valence guard is the difference: Ali Baba carts home *real* gold (real means, really possessed), where the counterfeit grants the *feeling* of abundance with nothing behind it — the modern MLM / "boss-babe" / luxury-influencer pipeline, which sells the lifestyle photo and the starter kit to those who fund the seller's actual plenty.

## The evidence

The clean specimen of abundance's **scarcity-into-plenty** shape: a man held in poverty is delivered into a catalogued mass of described wealth, and the payoff is the lingering, savored plenty itself — not a lover (being-desired), not a higher social price (repricing), but the *things*, named and heaped. The plenty is **terminal** here, which is what isolates abundance as a spine: no romance and no revenge live at the payoff beat — the savored wealth *is* the event.

### Slot 1 — Scarcity (the want the plenty is priced against)

Ali Baba is introduced in poverty, his living scraped from daily labour:

> "lived in a very wretched habitation, and had no other means to maintain his wife and children but his daily labour of cutting wood" (ll. 7124–7125)

This is the remembered want that the plenty is felt *against* — the wish-valence guard. Without this contrast the cave would be set-dressing; with it, the heap is a wish.

### Slot 2 — The plenty really acquired (real means behind it, really possessed)

The wealth is not a feeling or a promise — Ali Baba physically enters, loads, and carts home **real** gold; the plenty rests on real means really possessed:

> "He never regarded the silver, but made the best use of his time in carrying out as much of the gold coin as he thought his three asses could carry." (ll. 7190–7192)

This is the slot the counterfeit skips: the genuine engine's plenty has actual wealth behind it.

### Slot 3 — Immersion in savored, catalogued plenty

The payoff is the described mass of wealth, itemized and heaped — abundance's signature move, the savored catalogue:

> "He saw all sorts of provisions, rich bales of silk stuff, brocade, and valuable carpeting, piled upon one another; gold and silver ingots in great heaps, and money in bags." (ll. 7182–7184)

> "He then emptied the bags, which raised such a great heap of gold as dazzled his wife's eyes" (ll. 7208–7209)

The catalogue lingers; the heap *dazzles*. The wish is the plenty, savored as such.

### Guard / distinctness — abundance, not repricing, not being-desired, not mastery

The wish-valence guard: the plenty is **priced against remembered scarcity** (felt as abundance only against the woodcutter's want) and **really possessed** (real gold, carted home), not merely displayed.

- **Not repricing.** No one re-prices Ali Baba's *worth*; the cave does not correct a social valuation on a different trait. The wish is the material plenty, not recognition.
- **Not being-desired.** There is no desirer and no relation at the payoff — the gold is wanted, not a lover. (Contrast *Aladdin*, #128, the rider, below.)
- **Not mastery.** The plenty is *found*, not *built* through a regimen — Ali Baba earns nothing by craft; he stumbles on "Open, Sesame." Abundance's middle is the plenty's *reality/possession*, not earned capability.

**Rider contrast — *Aladdin* (#128) sharpens the guard.** The same jewel-and-treasure imagery appears in *Aladdin*, but there the plenty is **instrumental**: the jewels are the bride-price to win the princess ("Ought I not to bestow the princess on one who values her at such a price?"), so abundance rides *being-desired*, marriage is the end, and the plenty is a means. Abundance isolates as a **spine** only when the plenty is *terminal* (Ali Baba) and reads as a *rider* when instrumental to a relational wish (Aladdin).

Honest caveat: Ali Baba's *tale* then pivots to a thriller (Cassim's death, Morgiana and the oil-jars), so abundance is the spine of the *wish/opening beat*, not the whole plot — still a near-pure specimen for the wish, exactly as the genuine specimen of an engine need not run the engine end to end.

### Result

All three slots fill; the wish is the savored, catalogued plenty, priced against scarcity and really possessed. Tagged **abundance** — the scarcity-into-plenty shape. The maximally-different second specimen, *A Little Princess* (#146), carries the same wish in a Victorian domestic-interior register ([A Little Princess](/cupel/works/a-little-princess/)).
