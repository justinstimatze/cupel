- **purity/contamination** · content · also-runs · ✓ partial-refusal — at the *complicity-as-default-condition* register. ### Engine 1 — Order/legibility (totalizing systemic frame)

**Slot 1 — the unrevealed cosmos.** Race as undertheorized social organizer — the reader who has not yet recognized race-as-construct's structural pervasiveness.

**Slot 2 — the legible framework.** The book's analytical move: race as social construct that nonetheless *shapes every aspect* of life-trajectory:

> "race as a social construct has profound significance and shapes every aspect of our lives.… Race will influence whether we will survive our birth, where we are most likely to live, which schools we will attend, who our friends and partners will be, what careers we will have, how much money we will earn, how healthy we will be, and even how long we can expect to live" (Ch. 1)

**Slot 3 — the revealed order.** The phenomenology of whiteness made legible — the defensive patterns named as structural rather than individual-character.

Order/legibility's cluster-canonical signature in DiAngelo: the framework is systematic, the analytic work substantively operationalized, the slot-3 revealed-order does not require identity-purchase to access. The leg fills at the **partial-refusal pole**.

### Engine 2 — Purity/contamination (complicity-as-default; ongoing cleansing)

**Slot 1 — the contaminated condition.** Whiteness as structurally socialized into superiority-fragility — the contamination is the default condition, not an aberration:

> "Socialized into a deeply internalized sense of superiority that we either are unaware of or can never admit to ourselves, we become highly fragile in conversations about race.… White fragility is not weakness per se. In fact, it is a powerful means of white racial control" (Ch. 1)

**Slot 2 — the cleansing practice.** Named explicitly as ongoing, never completed — the partial-refusal pole signature:

> "what we need to be doing for the rest of our lives: engaging in ongoing self-awareness, continuing education, relationship building, and actual antiracist practice" (Ch. 1)

**Slot 3 — the clean state.** Not offered as achievable — the book *refuses* the cleansing-as-purchaseable move. The state offered is the practitioner inside the ongoing practice, not the practitioner who has completed it.

Purity/contamination's cluster-canonical signature in DiAngelo: the cleansing is named as lifelong work. The pure-counterfeit-pole move (identity as cleansed via reading the book) is structurally refused. The leg fills at the **partial-refusal pole**.

### Engine 3 — Virtue of defeat (moral standing held now via accepted position)

**Slot 1 — the unaccepted condition.** The white reader who defends against the complicity-position — the defensiveness itself read as confirmation of the position-being-defended-against.

**Slot 2 — the accepted position.** The text's pedagogical move asks the white reader to accept the structural-complicity position as the route — moral elevation granted on the bearer's own authority through the accepted position-against-the-system:

> *(from the Foreword endorsement)*: "the incentive for white engagement in racial justice work is ultimately self-liberation"

**Slot 3 — the elevated standing.** Held now via the position-taking, conditional on the ongoing slot-2 work.

Virtue of defeat's cluster-canonical signature in DiAngelo: the moral elevation is bearer-realizable (engine-defining property) and is conditional on slot-2 work the text names honestly. The leg fills at the **partial-refusal pole**.

### Engine 4 — Belonging (the cleared-eye in-group)

**Slot 1 — the exclusion.** The white progressive who has been outside the analytical-vocabulary community — the audience the book is addressed to, explicitly:

> "This book is intended for us, for white progressives" (Introduction)

> "white progressives who so often—despite our conscious intentions—make life so difficult for people of color" (Introduction)

**Slot 2 — the group's substantive practice.** The shared analytical vocabulary + the ongoing practice formed into community labor — the "we" the text addresses is formatted as a substantive practitioner-community, not a vocabulary-membership.

**Slot 3 — the in-group identity.** Cleared-eye recognition of the patterns, held inside the practitioner-community.

Belonging's cluster-canonical signature in DiAngelo: the in-group identity is framed as ongoing labor. Vocabulary-adoption alone does not confer membership in DiAngelo's frame. The leg fills at the **partial-refusal pole**.
