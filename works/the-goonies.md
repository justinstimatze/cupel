## The reading

**The bead.** A gang of misfit kids whose homes are about to be bulldozed follow a pirate's treasure map through booby-trapped tunnels and save their neighborhood — one last adventure together.

**Engines**
- **belonging** · content · spine · ~ — the Goonies themselves: the band of outcast kids, the last-summer-together loyalty ("Goonies never say die"), the found-family of the misfit crew is the heart the adventure hangs on.
- **order/legibility** · content · also-runs · ~ — the treasure-hunt click: One-Eyed Willy's map, the riddles, the booby-traps decoded step by step — a hidden world read into a route, the puzzle resolving.
- **abundance** · content · also-runs · ~ — Willy's treasure, the literal payoff that pays off the foreclosure and saves the homes; scarcity-to-rescue in a chest of pirate gold.

**The bundle.** The kids'-adventure bundle: belonging (the gang) on a treasure-hunt (order/legibility) for the abundance that saves their world — the misfit crew's loyalty the through-line.

**Dual-use read.** Order/legibility's counterfeit is the decoder-fantasy (a clever map explains everything); The Goonies keeps it benign — the map is real, the method honest, the treasure earned through the gauntlet. Abundance's counterfeit (the windfall that fixes everything) is softened by being *shared* and in service of the homes, not hoarded.

**Verdict.** A belonging-spined kids' adventure — the found-family crew, the decoded treasure-hunt, the windfall that saves the neighborhood.
