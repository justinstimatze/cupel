## The reading

**The bead.** Samuel Rowbotham's 1865 anonymous foundational text of the modern flat-earth movement, published under the pen-name "Parallax" and structured as an "experimental inquiry" against the orthodox spherical-cosmology of mainstream Victorian astronomy. The catalog's clearest **single-engine canon-founding specimen** of the *physical-cosmology-empirically-defended* sub-register of order/legibility — the sub-register subsequently inherited by Voliva's cult-mode flat-earth (1914-1942, see [The Christian Catholic Apostolic Church / Zion, Illinois under Voliva](/cupel/works/voliva-zion/)), by Aum's scientific-pose mode at distinct register ([Destroying the World to Save It (Aum Shinrikyō)](/cupel/works/aum-shinrikyo/)), and by modern distributed protected-world-mode flat-earth communities. Rowbotham predates all the cluster-specimens that adopt his sub-register by ~50-160 years; the sub-register is **older than any cluster that runs it**.

**Engines** — Rowbotham runs ONE engine substantively. Honest single-engine reading; *not a cluster-cluster specimen*.

- **order/legibility** · content · spine · ~ — at the **physical-cosmology-empirically-defended** sub-register (canon-founding). Slot-1: the orthodox spherical Earth cosmology rendered as *theoretical system imposed against direct observation* — "All theories are of this character—'supposing instead of inquiring, imagining systems instead of learning from observation and experience the true constitution of things'" (l. 184). Slot-2: the *zetetic method* — "the term 'zetetic' is derived from the Greek verb *zeteo*; which means to search or examine—to proceed only by inquiry" — applied via the **Bedford Level experiment** (ll. 246-254): boat-and-flag observation across 6 statute miles of standing canal water, observer with telescope at 8" above water; flag visible throughout whole distance; therefore canal-water is *plane, not convex*; therefore Earth is *not a globe*. Slot-3: the Earth-as-stationary-plane cosmology as the revealed-truth the method recovers. The engine's slot-2 *defended via public empirical style instrument* register at its canon-founding form. Methodologically significant: Rowbotham aligns his project with **inquiry-against-theorising**, framing flat-earth advocacy as the *epistemologically-virtuous* counter to mainstream science's *system-imposing speculation*. This is the canon-founding move that gets weaponised in cluster-form: when Voliva (1914+) and modern flat-earth communities adopt the sub-register, they inherit the *zetetic-method-as-epistemic-authority* framing alongside the empirical-instrument apparatus.

**NOT present**: apotheosis, mastery, impunity. No closed community; no founder-followers structure (Rowbotham operates as itinerant lecturer publishing under a pen-name); no in-group lifestyle-laws; no ascent-ladder; no exemption-from-norms claim. The work is *single-engine* — the order/legibility sub-register operating without the other cluster-legs.

**The bundle.** Single-engine specimen. *Not a cluster specimen.* The cluster forms only when this single-engine canon-founding text gets combined with apotheosis + mastery + impunity in a closed-community setting (Voliva), or when it gets combined with apotheosis + impunity-from-mainstream-norms in a distributed-no-leader setting (modern flat-earth via QAnon-shaped protected-world cluster). Rowbotham himself runs only the order/legibility engine.

**Dual-use read.** *Single-engine counterfeit at the order/legibility register*, with the *physical-cosmology-empirically-defended* sub-register's signature slot-2 deficit: the empirical-defense-apparatus operates as authority-display rather than as actual epistemological accountability. The Bedford Level experiment, taken at face value, ignores atmospheric refraction (well-documented by Victorian astronomy at the time of Rowbotham's writing; refraction in low layers near water surfaces routinely permits direct observation of objects beyond the geometric horizon, which is in fact what enabled the boat-with-flag to be visible throughout the 6 miles). Wallace's later 1870 re-run of the Bedford Level experiment, with marker poles at known heights placed at the canal's midpoint, recovered the curvature signature directly. Rowbotham's slot-2 deficit is *the empirical apparatus that looks-like-evidence but cannot survive controlled re-run* — the canon-founding form of the sub-register's signature deficit.

**Consumption.** Foundational text of the modern flat-earth movement; consumed continuously from 1865 onward via subsequent printings, William Carpenter's American republications (Baltimore 1885), Lady Blount's *Earth not a Globe Review* (UK periodical), Thomas Winship's *Zetetic Cosmogony*, and via Rowbotham's own touring lecture circuit. Voliva re-published Carpenter's *One Hundred Proofs that the Earth is not a Globe* (Rowbotham's American follower) at Zion (see [The Christian Catholic Apostolic Church / Zion, Illinois under Voliva](/cupel/works/voliva-zion/)). Now PD-fetched from Project Gutenberg as PG #69892 (released January 2023).

**Verdict.** The catalog's clearest **single-engine canon-founding specimen** of the physical-cosmology-empirically-defended order/legibility sub-register.
