## The reading

**The bead.** A 1973 short philosophical-fiction work depicting the utopian city of Omelas during its summer festival — and revealing that the city's prosperity depends on the perpetual misery of a single child kept in a basement closet, which every citizen knows about and accepts as the price — and the few who, knowing, *walk away* from the city, alone, into the dark. The catalog's cleanest specimen of **single-engine refusal at short-story scale**.

**Engines**
- **liberation/autonomy** · content · spine · ~ — at the *refusing the social contract built on the named victim* backing. Slot-1 (the imposed Omelas-contract: enjoy this beauty in full awareness that it requires the child's suffering); slot-2 (the walker's knowing-departure, in full acceptance that the alternative-place may not exist); slot-3 (walking-away as the engine's consummation — no destination promised, but the refusal complete). The refusal is itself the liberation; the destination is unknown and may not exist.

**The bundle.** Single-engine, *deliberately so*. Le Guin's structural commitment is to give the engine no other work — the moral weight rests entirely on the walker's choice. **Slot-proven specimen of a refusal-mode that the existing refusal-catalog does not perfectly fit**: not antagonist-mode (the city is not dramatized as the engine's enemy — Le Guin's prose is at pains to make Omelas *believable as actually good*); not adjacent-mode (no other engine fills the void); not model-refusal (the work operates within the wish-fulfillment frame). This is a fourth single-engine refusal mode: **refusal of the contract while staying in the frame**, where the engine runs at slot-3 by refusing a prior contract that the rest of the catalog accepts. Worth adding to the refusal-catalog.

**Dual-use read.** *Substantively enabling; the work has functioned as a moral-touchstone for half a century.* The story's load-bearing claim — that some social goods are illegitimate at their cost — is a serious moral position the work holds open without resolving. The slot-2 deficit risk: the *aestheticization of walking-away* as identity-purchase (the trust-fund-radical posture; the *I-saw-through-the-system* signaling) — a counterfeit reading some critics have advanced. Le Guin's prose explicitly walks back this reading by emphasizing the walkers' destination is *unknown to the writer*, and they walk *alone*. Value-flow: clean enabling at the substantive content.

**The famous N. K. Jemisin response.** Jemisin's "The Ones Who Stay and Fight" (2018) is the canonical cluster-adjacent-narrative-refusal of Omelas's premise — argues the walker's choice is insufficient, that staying-and-fighting is the substantive moral response. Cupel's catalog already has [Emergency Skin](/cupel/works/emergency-skin/) running adjacent moves; Jemisin-Omelas would be worth adding to extend the conversation.

**Verdict.** Foundational specimen of single-engine refusal-of-the-contract, slot-proven at the philosophical-fiction-short-story register. Identifies a *fourth* single-engine refusal mode the existing refusal catalog should incorporate. Le Guin's structural move is methodologically pure.
