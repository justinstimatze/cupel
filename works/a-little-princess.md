## The evidence

The maximally-different second specimen of abundance: the same scarcity-into-plenty wish as *Ali Baba* (#20916), but in a Victorian domestic-interior register rather than an oral folk-tale treasure-cave. Sara Crewe, fallen from wealth into a cold, bare attic and real hunger, wakes to find the garret transformed overnight into a savored catalogue of warmth and plenty. This is the **spine** version of the wish the [*ACOTAR*](/cupel/works/a-court-of-thorns-and-roses/) fill ran only as a romance *rider* — here the described luxury is terminal, the payoff itself, with no lover and no social re-valuation at the beat.

### Slot 1 — Scarcity (the want the plenty is priced against)

The attic is bare and cold; Sara's want is acute and named:

> "There was a rusty grate, an old iron bedstead, and a hard bed covered with a faded coverlet." (ll. 2819–2820)

> "I'm cold; I'm wet; I'm starving to death." (ll. 3903–3904)

The wish-valence guard is built into the specimen: the *same* rusty grate becomes the glowing fire, the *same* hard faded bed the satin quilt. The plenty is felt precisely against this remembered want.

### Slot 2 — The plenty really acquired (real, conferred by real means)

The scene insists the plenty is **real**, not a dream or a wish — and it is backed by a real giver (the later-revealed benefactor Mr. Carrisford, whose real fortune funds the gift):

> "To the little girl in the attic. From a friend." (l. 6217)

> "They are as real as we are. The Magic has come and done it, Becky, while we were asleep" (ll. 6245–6246)

This is the slot the counterfeit skips: the abundance has actual means behind it; it is delivered, not merely promised.

### Slot 3 — Immersion in savored, catalogued plenty

The payoff is the long itemized catalogue of restored comfort — abundance's signature savored list:

> "In the grate there was a glowing, blazing fire; on the hob was a little brass kettle hissing and boiling; spread upon the floor was a thick, warm crimson rug; before the fire a folding-chair, unfolded, and with cushions on it" (ll. 6168–6170)

> "a luxurious wadded robe of crimson silk" (l. 6233)

> "rich, hot, savory soup, which was a meal in itself, and sandwiches and toast and muffins enough for both of them" (ll. 6280–6281)

The catalogue is the wish. The fusion line names the scarcity-against-plenty contrast in a single breath — the plenty drawing in her "hungry senses":

> "drew her into the warm, glowing midst of things which made her brain reel and her hungry senses faint" (ll. 6241–6243)

### Guard / distinctness — abundance, not being-desired, not repricing, not redemption

The wish-valence guard: the plenty is **priced against remembered scarcity** and **really conferred** (real means behind it), not performed.

- **Not being-desired.** The benefactor is *anonymous* — "I don't know who it is" — so there is no desirer-beloved relation, no being-wanted-*as-she-is* by a specific high-value other. The savored content is the warmth and plenty; the "friend" is the (unknown) *source*, not a lover. The relation is thin and one-way; the wish is the things.
- **Not repricing.** The transformation is private — Sara and Becky alone in the attic — and re-prices no social worth. The book's public restoration (Sara recognized, returned to fortune) comes *later*; abundance is the spine of *this scene*.
- **Not redemption.** Sara did no wrong; there is nothing to atone for. She is wronged, not guilty.

Honest caveat: *A Little Princess* as a whole runs a restoration/repricing arc (Sara is eventually restored to her fortune and publicly recognized), so abundance is the spine of the **transformation beat**, not the whole plot — the same discipline as *Ali Baba*'s "spine of the wish, not the whole tale." The beat itself is a near-pure abundance specimen.

A note on the genuine/counterfeit edge this specimen sits on: Sara half-reaches for the counterfeit's logic — "If I can only keep on thinking it... I don't care!" — *as if* thinking could make plenty real. But the text refuses it: the Magic is a real benefactor with real means, not mind-power. That is exactly the line the counterfeit (Wattles' *The Science of Getting Rich*) erases — riches by thought alone, the means denied.

### Result

All three slots fill; the savored plenty is terminal, priced against scarcity, really conferred. Tagged **abundance** — the scarcity-into-plenty shape, in a register maximally different from the folk-tale treasure-cave primary ([Ali Baba and the Forty Thieves" (The Arabian Nights: Their Best-known Tales)](/cupel/works/ali-baba/)).
