## The reading

**The bead.** A team pulls off the literally impossible — the heist, the infiltration, the stunt no one could survive — and the pleasure is watching skill and trust execute a plan against the clock.

**Engines**
- **mastery** · content · spine · ~ — the franchise *is* virtuoso competence: the elaborate heist-and-infiltration executed flawlessly, the impossible stunt landed; the payout is expertise performed at the edge of the possible.
- **belonging** · content · also-runs · ~ — the IMF team, the loyalty under pressure ("we are the IMF"), the crew that won't leave one another behind — the found-family the impossible plan runs on.
- **security/safety** · content · also-runs · ~ — the standing stakes are catastrophe averted (the bomb, the pathogen, the rogue network); the defense that holds.

**The bundle.** A mastery spine (the impossible executed) carried by a loyal team (belonging) against a world-ending threat (security/safety) — competence + crew + stakes.

**Dual-use read.** Mastery's counterfeit is the competence-shortcut; M:I is the enabling case in spectacle form — the plan is shown, rehearsed, and goes wrong (the improvisation is the thrill), so the skill reads as worked, not granted.

**Verdict.** A mastery spine with a team-loyalty heart — competence-as-spectacle, the heist-thriller in its purest blockbuster form.
