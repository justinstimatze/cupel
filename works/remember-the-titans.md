## The reading

**The bead.** A newly integrated football team, thrown together across a town's racial divide, is forged into brothers — and an unbeaten season makes the bond undeniable.

**Engines**
- **belonging** · content · spine · ~ — the engine is the team becoming a family across the thing that should divide it: the camp, "left side, strong side," Gerry and Julius's brotherhood, a found-kinship that outgrows the town's hostility. The payout is the bond, not just the wins.
- **mastery** · content · also-runs · ~ — the perfect season: discipline, the drills, the wishbone offense; capability earned under Boone's regimen, the proof the belonging makes possible.

**The bundle.** A belonging spine (brotherhood across the divide) with mastery (the unbeaten season) as its vindication — the sports win is how the town is *made* to see the family the team already became.

**Dual-use read.** Belonging's counterfeit is the tribe that demands you erase yourself; the Titans run the opposite — the bond is forged *because* difference is faced, not denied, the in-group widened rather than walled.

**Verdict.** An underdog-sports belonging specimen where the real win is the brotherhood — the season the means by which it is recognized.
