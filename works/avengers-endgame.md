## The reading

**The bead.** After total defeat the survivors reassemble one last time to undo the loss — and the film pays out the family restored, the dead returned, and the torch passed at the cost of its founder's life.

**Engines**
- **legacy/transcendence** · content · spine · ~ — the MCU's legacy film, built around endings and what outlives them: Tony Stark's "I am Iron Man" and self-sacrifice, Captain America handing the shield to Sam, the funeral on the lake — "part of the journey is the end." The payout is meaning that survives the bearer's death. (Spine genuinely contested with belonging.)
- **belonging** · content · also-runs · ~ — the team reassembled from scattered, broken survivors; "Avengers assemble" as the emotional climax. The found-family restored one final time is co-load-bearing with legacy.
- **homecoming/reunion** · content · also-runs · ~ — the un-snapping returns the lost (the portals, families reunited), and Cap's closing choice — to go back and live the life he missed with Peggy — is textbook reunion: a return to a prior place and love, refusing the better new world.
- **redemption** · content · also-runs · ~ — distributed across the cast: Thor (from failure back to "still worthy"), Nebula (killing her own past self), Hawkeye/Ronin (the killer pulled back by the mission). Not the viewer's spine, but a genuine recurring beat.

**The bundle.** The finale bundle — a film engineered to cash several emotional accounts at once: the family reassembled (belonging), the torch passed at mortal cost (legacy), the dead and the missed life returned (reunion), the fallen redeemed. **Apotheosis is tested and set aside** (Tony's gauntlet, Cap lifting Mjolnir are spectacle, not the wish paid out); **virtue-of-defeat too** (the first act stages the dignity of the defeated, but the film ultimately wins the loss *back*, so it is a transit, not the payout). Four engines clear the guard, not six.

**Dual-use read.** Legacy's counterfeit is the "your name will be remembered, your sacrifice will matter" pitch detached from any real deed (the martyr register, [counterfeit-catalog](/cupel/theory/counterfeit-catalog/)). Endgame sits on the enabling side — the legacy is bought with an actual, irreversible sacrifice — but it is also the franchise's most efficient machine for making a manufactured corporate finale *feel* like earned transcendence, the value-flow caution to hold.

**Consumption.** The event-film as belonging badge — "I was there opening night," the fandom as the found-family the film depicts.

**Verdict.** The leaderboard-breaker: a work that genuinely runs four engines at once, which is *why* it lands as it does — the clearest catalog evidence that engines compose, and that a finale is the form built to stack them.

**Discriminator note.** Per, Endgame's *plot mechanic* is sub-variant-C impunity (consequence-reversibility via time-travel to undo the Thanos snap). But the film insists the undoing has its own irreversible costs — Tony's death, Black Widow's sacrifice, Cap's choice to live the missed life. **This is sub variant C with cost priced in**: consequence-reversibility runs as plot-mechanism but the film refuses the impunity the audience-pose would naively get (the dead get returned, but some costs stay paid). The engine runs at *constrained-impunity* register, not the pure sub-variant-C of *Edge of Tomorrow* (no permanent cost) or *Back to the Future* (consequences fully editable). The discriminator note adds nuance: route-3 sub-variant C is a *spectrum* from pure (Edge of Tomorrow) to cost-priced-in (Endgame), and the spectrum-position affects the engine's audience-pose payout.
