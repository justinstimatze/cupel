## The evidence

**The bead.** That you can throw off a constraint imposed on you from outside — be owned no longer, and become your own.

### liberation/autonomy — slot-proof

The first of two maximally-different genuine specimens, chosen as the *maximal external-constraint* case (legal slavery) — the sharpest discriminator against unleashing, whose held-back is the protagonist's *own* restraint.

1. **Held back** *(the imposed constraint)* — owned, a thing in law: "left a slave—a slave for life—a slave in the hands of strangers" (1721); "_slave for life!_ Have not I as good a right to be free as you have?" (1498). The constraint is imposed by a system, not self-chosen.
2. **The backing** *(the real, costly release)* — the escape at mortal risk: "I had as well be killed running as die standing… one hundred miles straight north, and I am free!… It cannot be that I shall live and die a slave" (2155-2158); afterward hunted by "money-loving kidnappers," the motto "Trust no man!" (3256+).
3. **The payoff** *(self-ownership seized)* — freedom savored as such: "a moment of the highest excitement I ever experienced… I felt like one who had escaped a den of hungry lions" (3239-3244).

**Isolation clinchers.**
- **vs unleashing** — Douglass discharges no held-back *capacity*; the slot-1 is *external* ownership, and slot-3 lifts a *constraint* rather than releasing a pent power.
- **vs impunity** — the payoff is being *unowned*, not acting *unaccountably* (no watcher/judge evaded).
- **vs security — decided on the page** — the freedom-moment "very soon subsided; and I was again seized with a feeling of great insecurity and loneliness. I was yet liable to be taken back" (3244-3246): he is *free yet unsafe*, so the payoff lands at the escape, before and independent of safety. Freedom-from-ownership ≠ safety-from-harm.
- **vs repricing/mastery** — the earlier Covey-fight beat ("a glorious resurrection… to the heaven of freedom," 2354-2355) confers *self-respect* — a slave "made a man" in spirit while still enslaved in fact — a different, earlier beat from the literal-freedom slot-3.

**Criterion check.** slot-3 ("I am free / my own master") is **bearer-realizable** — you can *seize* freedom (escape, revolt) — predicting an **engine** with a standalone counterfeit. Confirmed: manufactured liberation / "forced to be free" ([counterfeit-catalog](/cupel/theory/counterfeit-catalog/)).

**Verdict.** liberation/autonomy isolates here on the maximal external-constraint case. With *A Doll's House* (second, maximally-different specimen) and the counterfeit standing alone (Rousseau), liberation/autonomy is a confirmed engine.
