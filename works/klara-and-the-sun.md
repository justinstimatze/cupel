## The evidence

The companion to [Never Let Me Go](/cupel/works/never-let-me-go/) at Ishiguro's signature register — *caretaking-and-being-needed by something whose personhood is itself the novel's-substantive-question* — extended into a near-future-SF dystopia where the structurally-disposable narrator is *not* a clone whose body becomes organs (Tommy and Kathy's register), but an Artificial Friend whose obsolescence is a *slow fade in the Yard* (Klara's register). Three engines fill against Ishiguro's text on the verbatim layer: caretaking/being-needed (spine), virtue of defeat (Klara's substantive religion as the engine's slot-2 mechanism rendered without confirmation-or-disconfirmation), and liberation/autonomy (the lifted-vs-unlifted social structure as the unresolved-injustice the novel sustains throughout).

### Engine 1 — Caretaking/being-needed (Klara as the structurally-disposable narrator)

**Slot 1 — existence only when purchased and only as functional.** Klara's pre-Josie store-front waiting: positioned in the window for the Sun's nourishment but also for *being-chosen*. The store's structural condition — Manager allocating positions, AFs anxious about being passed-over, the structural fear of obsolescence:

> "I realize Rex wouldn't have been the only one. But officially, it wasn't an issue at all – every one of us had specifications that guaranteed we couldn't be affected by factors such as our positioning within a room. Even so, an AF would feel himself growing lethargic after a few hours away from the Sun, and start to worry there was something wrong with him – that he had some fault unique to him and that if it became known, he'd never find a home." (l. 189)

The other-AF model — AFs on the sidewalk turning away from the store-front because they are old models who fear *their* children's replacement of them — gives the cluster's slot-1 its register-specific intensification:

> "Then as I continued to watch the outside, another possibility came to me: that the AFs weren't embarrassed, but were afraid. They were afraid because we were new models, and they feared that before long their children would decide it was time to have them thrown away, to be replaced by AFs like us." (l. 323)

**Slot 2 — Klara's sustained patient attentive presence with Josie.** The engine's mechanism is the *quality of attention* Klara brings to Josie's condition — the carefully-tracked physical movements, Josie's varying strength, the moods of the Mother, the structural-meaning of Rick's relationship with Josie. The novel renders the engine's slot-2 at the *attentional-precision register* — the catalog's clearest single-text specimen of the engine's slot-2 *expressed as the substantive presence of the carer*. Klara herself articulates the engine's slot-2 in her first interaction with Josie's mother at the kitchen island:

> "Thank you for choosing me." (l. 4391, condensed from Klara's closing-scene exchange with Josie)

**Slot 3 — the engine's payoff as honest acceptance of being needed and then no longer needed.** The novel's terminal scene — Klara in the Yard, watching the construction crane and the Sun's journeys, allowing her memories to overlap as her functioning slowly degrades — is the engine's slot-3 *delivered as accepting-the-slow-fade*:

> "Even so, such composite memories have sometimes filled my mind so vividly, I've forgotten for long moments that I am, in reality, sitting here in the Yard, on this hard ground." (final chapter, the Yard scene)

The Mother's earlier defense of Klara against Mr Capaldi's reverse-engineering proposal articulates the engine's slot-3 *register-name* from inside the novel:

> "'Klara deserves better than that.' … 'Klara deserves better. She deserves her slow fade.'" (final chapter, Capaldi-confrontation scene)

The engine's slot-3 *delivered as the dignified-decline of the carer who has done the work of caring*. Ishiguro's signature *caretaking/being-needed* register: the carer's slot-3 is not *thanked-and-rewarded-and-retained*; it is *seen-clearly-and-released*.

### Engine 2 — Virtue of defeat (Klara's substantive religion as the engine's slot-2 mechanism rendered without confirmation-or-disconfirmation)

**Slot 1 — Josie's illness and the Mother's contingency plan.** Josie's sickness is the engine's standard slot-1: a condition the engine's standard slot-2 mechanisms (medicine, the "lifting" the novel posits as the cause of her sickness) cannot fix. Mr Capaldi's plan — that Klara would *continue Josie* if Josie dies, taking over her body's portrait-form and Klara's substantive-attention as the substantive replacement — is the engine's slot-1 *intensified to the substantive level the novel operates at*. The Father articulates the AI-personhood question Mr Capaldi's plan rests on at his most direct:

> "I think I hate Capaldi because deep down I suspect he may be right. That what he claims is true. That science has now proved beyond doubt there's nothing so unique about my daughter, nothing there our modern tools can't excavate, copy, transfer. That people have been living with one another all this time, centuries, loving and hating each other, and all on a mistaken premise. A kind of superstition we kept going while we didn't know better." (l. 4189)

**Slot 2 — Klara's substantive religion as the engine's mechanism.** Klara's religion is the cluster's slot-2 in its most substantive form — *the Sun as substantive source of nourishment and special help*, with the *Beggar Man precedent* (the Sun's having given Beggar Man "special nourishment" after the Cootings Machine pollution ceased) as the engine's slot-2 *historical-precedent the mechanism rests on*:

> "I thought too about the time the Sun had given his special nourishment to Beggar Man and his dog, and considered the important differences between his circumstances and Josie's. For one thing, many passers-by had known Beggar Man, and when he'd become weak, he'd done so in a busy street, visible to taxi drivers and runners. Any of these people might have drawn the Sun's attention to his condition and that of his dog. Even more significantly, I remembered what had been happening not long before the Sun had given his special nourishment to Beggar Man. The Cootings Machine had been making its awful Pollution, obliging even the Sun to retreat for a time, and it had been during the fresh new era after the dreadful machine had gone away that the Sun, relieved and full of happiness, had given his special help." (l. 2179)

The Cootings-Machine-destruction commitment — Klara's plan to draw the Sun's special help by destroying the source-of-pollution that offends him — is the engine's slot-2 *commitment-by-the-carer*. Klara's first appeal to the Sun in the barn (l. 3101) is the cluster's slot-2 *delivered at the substantive religious-experience register*.

**Slot 3 — Josie recovers, but the recovery is *not* clearly attributable to Klara's appeal.** Ishiguro's structural commitment: the engine's slot-3 *delivered while honoring the unverifiability of the slot-2 mechanism*. Josie gets better; the Sun *may* have intervened; the substantive-religion is *neither dismissed as superstition nor confirmed as truth*. The engine pays out *honoring substantive religion without confirming its mechanism* — virtue of defeat's slot-3 *at the unverifiable register*.

### Engine 3 — Liberation/autonomy (the lifted-vs-unlifted social structure as the unresolved-injustice the novel sustains)

**Slot 1 — the lifted-vs-unlifted social-stratification.** The genetic-enhancement procedure ("lifting") that elite parents subject their children to, with Josie's illness implied as the result of complications from her lifting and Rick's lower-class status implied by his not being lifted. The novel's structural-injustice slot-1 — *children sorted by genetic-modification access into futures the social order assigns at birth*.

**Slot 2 — Rick's struggle for college admission as the engine's mechanism.** The interaction-meeting (l. 1077–) and the Atlas-Brookings effort (l. 2939–) render the engine's slot-2 — *the unlifted child's attempt to access the futures the lifting-system reserves for the lifted* — with the structural-difficulty made legible without polemic.

**Slot 3 — the engine's slot-3 *withheld at the level the engine conventionally requires*.** The novel does *not* deliver liberation — Rick's college-application outcome is left ambiguous, the lifted-vs-unlifted system remains intact, and the structural-injustice the novel rendered for nine-tenths of its pages is *not resolved* at the closing. The engine's slot-3 *withheld as Ishiguro's structural commitment* — the catalog's literary-fiction-register specimen of *liberation/autonomy's structural-injustice rendered as substantively unresolved*.
