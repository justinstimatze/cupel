## The reading

**The bead.** A rage-contagion empties Britain, and the survivors' flight toward safety ends at a sanctuary that turns out to be the real infection — men who have decided the rules are gone.

**Engines**
- **security/safety** · content · spine · ~ — the threat (the Infected, the turn in seconds) drives a flight toward a promised military blockade; the spine is the search for a defense that holds. It is found false (the soldiers are the trap) and then seized back by force.
- **purity/contamination** · content · also-runs · ~ — the Rage virus is a literal contaminant, blood-borne, the clean-vs-infected line organizing every scene. But the film's reversal is the purity-counterfeit's: the soldiers who most confidently sort clean-from-infected — purging the Infected as vermin while planning to rape the women "to repopulate" — are themselves the monstrous thing. Contamination is shown to be moral, not viral.
- **impunity** · content · also-runs · ~ — the apocalypse-as-license register: with civilization gone, the soldiers' "there are no more rules" is the consequence-removal wish, accountability deleted by catastrophe.

**The bundle.** The contagion-siege bundle: security chased across a defiled landscape, the clean/infected line drawn and then turned back on the men who drew it most eagerly.

**Dual-use read.** Same core as the zombie vein: the Infected are the purity-spiral's object (irreversibly defiled, killable without weight), and Boyle — like Romero — names the counterfeit by handing it to the villains. The "purge the irreversibly-contaminated" logic (the Madison Grant register, [counterfeit-catalog](/cupel/theory/counterfeit-catalog/)) is voiced not by the heroes but by the soldiers who use it to license their own atrocity; the film's gratification (survival, the final rescue) is kept separate from that logic.

**Verdict.** The contagion variety of the vein: purity/contamination as the organizing clean-vs-infected logic, run as critique, with security/safety the bright wish the survivors actually chase.
