## The reading

**The bead.** A prince returns to claim his father's throne and the mantle of a nation's protector — and must prove the inheritance is his to carry.

**Engines**
- **legacy/transcendence** · content · spine · ~ — the throne of Wakanda and the mantle of the Black Panther, passed down the royal line; the ancestral plane where kings rejoin their fathers; T'Challa carrying — and questioning — T'Chaka's legacy. The payout is the inheritance claimed and made worthy.
- **belonging** · content · also-runs · ~ — Wakanda as nation-as-family: Shuri, Ramonda, the Dora Milaje, the tribes; the hero embedded in a people, not a lone vigilante.
- **apotheosis** · content · also-runs · ~ — the heart-shaped herb confers the Black Panther's enhanced power; the ritual ascension to more-than-human.

**The bundle.** The throne bundle — an inherited mantle (legacy) carried on behalf of a people (belonging) and empowered by ritual ascension (apotheosis). **Homecoming was considered and cut**: T'Challa's return to be crowned reads as legacy (claiming the throne) more than reunion (returning to a prior place, refusing the new), so it folds into the spine rather than tagging separately. A clean three, not four.

**Dual-use read.** Legacy's counterfeit (the "your bloodline endures" pitch) is present, but the sharper read is Killmonger: he embodies the **liberation** register run dark — his grievance ("bury me in the ocean with my ancestors who jumped from the ships") is treated by the film as *just*, his method (seize power, arm the world) as monstrous. The film hands a real liberation-wish to the antagonist and asks the hero to absorb the cause while rejecting the violence — single-artifact dual-use, the villain's wish half-validated. Per the Lolita discipline, Killmonger's liberation is *his* goal, not the work's clean payout to the viewer, so it is discussed here, not tagged.

**Verdict.** A three-engine entry (legacy + belonging + apotheosis) — the superhero bundle stacking past a single engine, though it sits at three, not the four-plus of the finale. The Killmonger liberation-register is the card's richest dual-use, not a fourth tag.

**Discriminator note.** Per § route 3 sub-variants A + B, the heart-shaped herb (A) and vibranium-suit (B) supply impunity-shape mechanisms — but T'Challa runs *partial-installation* like Cap: he gets the enhanced body and the kinetic-absorption suit, but he gets hurt, is nearly killed by Killmonger, and the wish-payout is *the inheritance claimed and made worthy* (legacy), not *acts-without-typical-consequence* (impunity). Black Panther is route-3 sub-variant-A-and-B partial-installation case (the mechanisms are present; impunity doesn't claim spine).
