## The reading

**The bead.** Faulkner's 1929 modernist masterwork — entered public domain January 1, 2025 — structured in four sections each from a different perspective on the Compson family's decline across April 1928 and a flashback to 1910: Benjy's stream-of-consciousness rendering across multiple time-periods conflated in one mental-instant (Benjy is intellectually-disabled); Quentin's elaborate suicidal-day at Harvard on the day of his suicide; Jason's economically-grasping-and-bitter present-day rendering; and Dilsey-the-Black-cook's third-person final section — with the central figure of Caddy Compson (whose loss-of-innocence drives all three brothers' arcs) never given her own narrative voice. The catalog's clearest specimen of *legacy/transcendence-as-antagonist-mode + the Compson family decline rendered through modernist form*.

**Engines**
- **legacy/transcendence-antagonist-mode** · content · spine · ~ — at the *the Compson family legacy is the thing being lost and the novel renders the slow loss without redemption* register. Slot-1 (the Compson family's Mississippi-aristocratic former-status; Caddy's loss-of-innocence; Quentin's substantive-religious-incest-anxiety; the family's structural-economic-decline); slot-2 (Faulkner's *modernist form itself as the substantive decline rendering* — Benjy's confusion; Quentin's obsessive-time-meditation on the day-of-his-suicide; Jason's compounding-bitterness); slot-3 (Dilsey's section's substantive-Easter-Sunday-religious-presence as the closing — Dilsey's *"I seed de beginnin, en now I sees de endin"* verified verbatim against Gutenberg PG #75170, the January-2025 PD release). Methodologically the catalog's clearest American-literary specimen of *legacy as the antagonist mode form itself renders the decline* register, sibling to Sandman (legacy at office-survives-the-person register) at distinct register and to Watchmen (apotheosis-antagonist-mode) at distinct register.
- **virtue of defeat** · content · also-runs · ~ — at the *Caddy's-loss-and-Quentin's-suicide-cannot-be-undone* register. The novel's structural commitment is to *honoring the losses substantively across multiple perspectives* without softening into resolution.
- **the double life** · content · also-runs · ~ — at the *each-narrator's rendering of the same events as substantively different* register. The modernist-multiple-perspective structure operates the double-life engine at the *narrator's rendering as substantively distinct from other narrators' rendering* register.

**The bundle.** A multi-engine modernist novel running legacy-antagonist-mode + virtue-of-defeat + the double life at the *Compson family decline rendered through multiple perspectives and modernist form* register. **Methodologically significant** for the catalog as the clearest foundational American-modernist specimen of *the modernist-form-as-the-engine's-mechanism* — Faulkner's commitment to *making the form-itself the substantive content of the family-decline* is methodologically distinct from prior cupel modernist specimens (Pynchon's order/legibility-counterfeit-as-form; Saunders's polyphonic-bardo-form).

**Dual-use read.** *Clean enabling.* Faulkner's structural commitment to *rendering the Compson decline substantively across all four perspectives without softening* is the substantive content. The slot-2 deficit risk shows where contemporary cultural reception consumed *the Faulkner-aesthetic* (the *I have read Sound and the Fury* prestige-purchase identity-signal among contemporary literary readers; the Nobel-Prize-and-canonical-author cultural-figure consumption) without the substantive engagement with the modernist form itself as the engine's-mechanism. Value-flow: clean enabling at source.

**Consumption.** Substantial American-literary-canonical footprint; consistent presence on greatest-American-novels lists; foundational influence on subsequent stream-of-consciousness and Southern-Gothic and modernist canon; Faulkner's 1949 Nobel Prize substantively-tied to the Sound-and-the-Fury reception. The work's January-2025 entry into public domain extends the consumption-layer reception.

**Verdict.** Foundational American-modernist specimen of legacy-antagonist-mode + virtue-of-defeat + the double life at modernist-form-as-engine-mechanism register. Methodologically significant for the catalog as the clearest foundational American specimen of *modernist-form-as-the-engine's-mechanism* — the form-itself renders the substantive content.
