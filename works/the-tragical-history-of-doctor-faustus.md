## The reading

**The bead.** To break the ceiling of mortal limits and seize god-like power itself — not earned by a regimen, not conferred by a destiny-mark, but *grasped*.

**Engines**
- **apotheosis** · content · spine · ✓ — Faustus, having exhausted every mortal discipline ("Yet art thou still but Faustus, and a man"), bargains for deity ("A sound magician is a mighty god"); the power is poured in by the pact (granted, not built through a regimen → **not mastery**) and grasped by his own ambition with no mark or chosen-ness (seized, not conferred → **not election**); he commands Mephistophilis and conjures Helen's shade.

**Dual-use read.** Apotheosis's counterfeit is **"realize your own divinity" — the unlock-your-god-mode grift**: *you are already divine/limitless; claim it and the powers attend you.* The documented ancestor is Ralph Waldo Trine's *In Tune with the Infinite* (#23559), the New-Thought ur-source behind modern manifesting / law-of-attraction (and the *Limitless* "a protocol grants the ascent" pitch). The difference is the wish-valence guard: Faustus's godhood is bought with a *real, costly ascent* (the pact, and damnation — the engine **enables** the fantasy of transcending limits), where the counterfeit grants slot-3 power by *mere realization* and skips the slot-2 ascent entirely (**substitutes** — godhood on credit, with failure routed back to insufficient belief, the manifesting bind). Value-flow call is subjective, per the README — Faustus sits on the enabling side (the ascent and its price are dramatized in full).

**Consumption.** Light — a canon/erudition badge ("I've read Marlowe"); the work's force is at the content layer.

**Verdict.** Apotheosis's public-domain specimen and the one that controls the election leak [ACOMAF](/cupel/works/a-court-of-mist-and-fury/) left open: god-like power with *no mark*, grasped by will. The honest caveat (the now-familiar literary pattern): Marlowe **prosecutes** the wish — Faustus is damned, the deity proves hollow — so the *fantasy the work services* is unmistakably apotheosis while the *frame* condemns it, exactly as Jekyll/Hyde and [Monte Cristo](/cupel/works/the-count-of-monte-cristo/) pre-moralize unleashing. The un-moralized, savored form lives in modern genre ([ACOMAF](/cupel/works/a-court-of-mist-and-fury/), LitRPG/cultivation); the slots are about wish *structure*, which fills clean.

## The evidence

Second specimen for the **apotheosis** candidate (first specimen: [A Court of Mist and Fury (ACOTAR #2)](/cupel/works/a-court-of-mist-and-fury/)). Its job, per the gate: be **maximally different** from ACOMAF *and* **control the election leak** the first specimen flagged. ACOMAF leaked election — Feyre's power came with being *Made* by the Cauldron, a mark. The second specimen must show power-ascension with **no mark, no chosen-ness** at all.

Maximal difference is total: where Maas gives a Made fae queen in a romance, Marlowe gives a Renaissance scholar who *bargains* for godhood in a damnation tragedy. The control is exact: Faustus is **not chosen** — he reaches for deity by his own will. If the apotheosis slots fill here, the gradient is independent of any election mark.

### Slot 1 — Ordinary limits

Faustus has exhausted every human discipline (logic, medicine, law, divinity) and each leaves him merely mortal — the limit chafes:

> "Yet art thou still but Faustus, and a man." (l. 155)

The specific limit is mortality itself — the thing no human art transcends:

> "Couldst thou make men to live eternally, / Or, being dead, raise them to life again, / Then this profession were to be esteem'd." (ll. 156–158)

### Slot 2 — The ascent (seized, not granted-by-a-mark)

The wish is named outright as **omnipotence / deity** — the apotheosis payoff in plain words:

> "O, what a world of profit and delight, / Of power, of honour, of omnipotence, / Is promis'd to the studious artizan!" (ll. 198–200)

> "A sound magician is a mighty god" (l. 207)

And — the election-control — the ascent is something Faustus **reaches for by his own will**, not a destiny conferred on him:

> "Ay, these are those that Faustus most desires." (l. 197)

The power arrives by pact (Mephistophilis is bound to serve him), so like ACOMAF it is **granted, not earned through a regimen** — the mastery-separation holds. But unlike ACOMAF there is **no Cauldron, no Making, no mark**: Faustus is not special and is not chosen; he grasps deity. That is precisely the election leak controlled.

### Slot 3 — Supreme power wielded

The payoff is command over nature and spirits — power without ceiling:

> "Shall I make spirits fetch me what I please, / Resolve me of all ambiguities, / Perform what desperate enterprise I will?" (ll. 236–238)

The pact delivers it: Faustus commands Mephistophilis, conjures, raises the dead shade of Helen. The wish the play is built to gratify is the god-like dominion itself.

### Wish-valence guard, and the leak controlled

The guard holds on both axes that define apotheosis. **Not mastery:** the power is poured in by the pact, not built by a regimen — Faustus does no training; he signs. (Consistent with ACOMAF, where the High Lords pour it in.) **Not election:** and here is the second specimen's whole contribution — the power is **seized by ambition, not conferred by a mark**. Feyre was *Made* (a latent election mechanism); Faustus is an ordinary scholar who *wants* deity and bargains for it. Across two maximally different works — one where the power is conferred, one where it is grasped — the payoff is the same (supreme, god-like power), and in neither is the payoff a *destiny*. Apotheosis fills independent of election.

### Result — and an honest pre-moralization

All three slots fill on a specimen maximally different from ACOMAF, and the **election leak is controlled**: god-like power with no mark, grasped by will. The guard holds (granted-not-earned → not mastery; power-not-destiny → not election). **Apotheosis now has two clean specimens with the guard**, and with the counterfeit shown — the "realize your own divinity" New-Thought grift (Trine's *In Tune with the Infinite*; modern manifesting / "unlock your god-mode," [counterfeit-catalog](/cupel/theory/counterfeit-catalog/)) — **apotheosis is a confirmed engine**.

The honest caveat (the now-familiar pattern): *Doctor Faustus* **moralizes its wish** — Faustus is damned, the Good Angel warns throughout, the deity proves hollow and the bill comes due. So the *fantasy the work services* (to grasp godhood, "a mighty god") is unmistakably apotheosis, but the work prosecutes it — exactly as *Jekyll/Hyde* and [*Monte Cristo*](/cupel/works/the-count-of-monte-cristo/) pre-moralize unleashing. The un-moralized, *savored* apotheosis lives in modern genre (ACOMAF, LitRPG/cultivation); the PD literary specimen comes with the hubris-punished frame. The slots are about wish *structure*, which fills; the moralization is logged, not disqualifying (it is the same shape mastery's and unleashing's literary specimens showed).
