## The reading

**The bead.** Barbara Kingsolver's 2022 Pulitzer-Prize-winning novel — a reimagining of Dickens's *David Copperfield* set in contemporary southern Appalachia (the actual Lee County, Virginia) — following Damon Fields, nicknamed "Demon Copperhead" for his copper-red hair, through his childhood in the foster-care system, his football-talent rise, his catastrophic OxyContin addiction across the late-1990s and 2000s opioid epidemic, and his recovery. The catalog's clearest contemporary specimen of *Appalachian-opioid-epidemic rendered at literary-fiction first-person narrator register*.

**Engines**
- **virtue of defeat** · content · spine · ~ — at the *the opioid epidemic cannot be fixed and Demon's recovery is substantive not redemptive* register. Slot-1 (Demon's structural-disadvantage from rural-Appalachian poverty; his mother's overdose-death; his foster-care experiences; his OxyContin addiction sourced from the documented Purdue Pharma marketing); slot-2 (his sustained slow honest patient work of recovery — Kingsolver's commitment to *rendering recovery as the substantive engine*); slot-3 (the novel ends with Demon working as an artist and surviving but not in a redeemed-condition; the friends and lovers who died across the novel are not undone). Kingsolver's structural commitment to *the opioid-epidemic as systemic-condition that the individual-protagonist's-survival cannot redeem* is the engine's content. Methodologically sibling to *Infinite Jest* at the substance-recovery substantive-rendering register, but with the broader political-economy critique foregrounded.
- **order/legibility** · content · also-runs · ~ — at the *the Purdue Pharma and the corporate economic structure as the substantive target* register. Kingsolver's structural commitment to *naming the OxyContin marketing program and the opioid-epidemic's corporate-economic causation* operates the engine at the *political economic critique rendered through personal narrative* register.

**The bundle.** A multi-engine contemporary American literary-fiction specimen running virtue-of-defeat + order/legibility at the *opioid epidemic rendered through personal narrative + corporate-causation-named* register. **Methodologically significant** for the cluster catalog as the clearest contemporary literary-fiction specimen of *corporate-cluster prosecuted-through-personal-narrative* — Kingsolver's commitment is to *making the corporate-economic causation visible through one rendered-life*. Sibling to Atlanta ([Atlanta](/cupel/works/atlanta-tv/)) at TV register and to Sucession ([Succession](/cupel/works/succession/)) at corporate-dynasty register as the catalog's contemporary specimens of corporate-cluster-counter-narrative at distinct registers.
