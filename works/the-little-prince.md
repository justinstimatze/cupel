## The reading

**The bead.** That a single creature you have *tamed* becomes uniquely yours and you uniquely responsible for it — and that seeing this is a recovery of the child's sight the adult world trains out of you.

**Engines**
- **belonging** · content · also-runs · ~ — the bonds (the rose, the fox, the pilot) are kin-bonds of a kind; the fox asks to be tamed so as not to be alone. But this only *partly* fills belonging: the wish is less "be taken into a tribe" than "matter uniquely to one other, and be responsible for it."

**The bundle.** Single dominant wish, weakly held by belonging — which is the point of flagging it.

**Dual-use read.** No clean single counterfeit, because the spine wish isn't cleanly one of the fifteen (below). The nearest dark edge is the sentimental-manipulation of the caretaking bond ("you're responsible for what you've tamed" weaponized into obligation/guilt) — but that's a gap-engine's counterfeit, not a confirmed one.

**Consumption.** The book as a token of tender-heartedness / recovered innocence — gifted to signal one still "sees with the heart."

**Verdict — COVERAGE GAP (the valuable kind).** The all-time best-seller list's purest taxonomy-stressor so far. The confirmed engines do **not** cleanly tag what *The Little Prince* services. Two candidate gradients it surfaces:
1. **caretaking / being-needed** — "you become responsible for what you have tamed"; the prince returns to his rose because *she needs him*. This is the tend-and-befriend wish (a drivermap candidate) — the wish to be *needed*, distinct from belonging (being taken in) and being-desired (being wanted). Flagged for slot-test.
2. **recovered innocence / wonder** — the wish to see as a child again, against the deadening adult ("grown-ups never understand anything by themselves"). Possibly its own gradient (nostalgia/innocence), possibly a value-stance rather than a wish-engine. Flagged, lower confidence.

A bestseller that resists the catalog is exactly the signal this pass exists to catch. NOT promoting either —as coverage-surfaced candidates. **Since: caretaking/being-needed graduated to a confirmed engine** (giver-side specimen *Charlotte's Web*); the prince's recovered-innocence/wonder wish remains the open stressor.
