## The evidence

Published November 27, 2023, six weeks after Andreessen's *Techno-Optimist Manifesto* (October 16, 2023). Buterin introduces d/acc (defensive / decentralization / differential / democracy acceleration) as a third position between the e/acc accelerationist pole (Andreessen) and the doomer pause/shutdown pole (Yudkowsky+Soares 2025). All four gravitational-center legs fill, at mixed registers across legs — breaking the within-strand pole-uniformity the original bistable-braid framing assumed.

### The three-position framing

> "Anti-technology view: safety behind, dystopia ahead. / Accelerationist view: dangers behind, utopia ahead. / My view: dangers behind, but multiple paths forward ahead: some good, some bad." (l. 47)

> "My own feelings about techno-optimism are warm, but nuanced. I believe in a future that is vastly brighter than the present thanks to radically transformative technology, and I believe in humans and humanity. I reject the mentality that the best we should try to do is to keep the world roughly the same as today but with less greed and more public healthcare. However, I think that not just magnitude but also direction matters." (l. 31-36)

The essay names Andreessen's manifesto and the OpenAI dispute as the inflection point.

### Apotheosis (spine)

Brain-computer interface + mind-upload as the defensive d/acc-version of transcendence:

> "Later stages of such a roadmap admittedly get weird. In addition to brain-computer interfaces, there are various paths to improving our brains directly through innovations in biology. An eventual further step, which merges both paths, may involve uploading our minds to run on computers directly. This would also be the ultimate d/acc for physical security: protecting ourselves from harm would no longer be a challenging problem of protecting inevitably-squishy human bodies, but rather a much simpler problem of making data backups." (l. 1119-1126)

The ascended state — cosmic-scale, brightest-star register:

> "And so it is my firm belief that, out of all the things that we have known and seen in our universe, we, humans, are the brightest star." (l. 1237-1238)

> "Two billion years from now, if the Earth or any part of the universe still bears the beauty of Earthly life, it will be human artifices like space travel and geoengineering that will have made it happen." (l. 1241-1244)

> "expanding humanity's reach even further to the planets and stars is deeply good, because I believe humanity is deeply good." (l. 1213-1215)

Apotheosis fills at the cluster's accelerationist-pole-equivalent register — Buterin shares Andreessen's vision of cosmic-scale human flourishing. The d/acc-distinctive register is defensive route to the same destination (mind-upload as data backups) rather than direct transcendence-of-limits.

### Order/legibility (also-runs)

The defensive-vs-offensive classification:

> "One frame to think about the macro consequences of technology is to look at the balance of defense vs offense. Some technologies make it easier to attack others… Others make it easier to defend, and even defend without reliance on large centralized actors." (l. 522-527)

> "This core idea, that some technologies are defense-favoring and are worth promoting, while other technologies are offense-favoring and should be discouraged, has roots in effective altruist literature under a different name: differential technology development." (l. 613-616)

The framework has actual content — Switzerland geography, Gitcoin negative-vote experiments, macro/micro/cyber/info defense taxonomy — that survives inspection. Less totalizing than Andreessen's techno-capital machine.

### Impunity (also-runs) — the d/acc-distinctive move

Buterin explicitly refuses the cluster's standard impunity move:

> "Across the board, I see far too many plans to save the world that involve giving a small group of people extreme and opaque power and hoping that they use it wisely. And so I find myself drawn to a different philosophy, one that has detailed ideas for how to deal with risks, but which seeks to create and maintain a more democratic world and tries to avoid centralization as the go-to solution to our problems." (l. 502-507)

> "These concerns are exactly why I think it would be ideal for a leading role in this path to be held by a security-focused open-source movement, rather than closed and proprietary corporations and venture capital funds." (l. 1134-1137)

The slot-3 is democratic governance preserved — not defection from precaution as moral imperative (Andreessen), not shut it down with nuclear treaty enforcement (Yudkowsky+Soares):

> "A defense-favoring world is a better world, for many reasons. First of course is the direct benefit of safety: fewer people die, less economic value gets destroyed, less time is wasted on conflict. What is less appreciated though is that a defense-favoring world makes it easier for healthier, more open and more freedom-respecting forms of governance to thrive." (l. 529-534)

The named counterfeit-operator inside the text is the centralized-power-grab. Partial-refusal-pole signature at the impunity-leg.

### Mastery (also-runs)

Defensive-technology development as the craft:

> "A d/acc message, one that says 'you should build, and build profitable things, but be much more selective and intentional in making sure you are building things that help you and humanity thrive', may be a winner." (l. 1163-1167)

Substantively grounded in cryptography, blockchain, public-good-coordination mechanisms. Closer to Ries 2011's partial-refusal startup-canon discipline than Andreessen's radical-competence Hero's Journey.

### The structural finding — per-leg pole-mixing

Yudkowsky+Soares 2025 runs uniformly partial-refusal. Andreessen 2023 runs uniformly pure-counterfeit. d/acc runs mixed across legs:

|---|---|---|---|
| Apotheosis | partial-refusal (surviving-humanity) | pure-counterfeit (transcendence) | pure-counterfeit-equivalent (brightest star, mind-upload) |
| Order/legibility | partial-refusal (honest prediction) | pure-counterfeit (techno-capital totalization) | partial-refusal-equivalent (defense-vs-offense, bounded) |
| Impunity | partial-refusal (by-negation) | pure-counterfeit (deceleration-as-murder) | partial-refusal (centralized-power-as-failure-mode) |

d/acc is structurally a cluster-bridging position, not a third pole on the bistable-braid spectrum. The bistable-braid framing is the special case for canonical-pair moments where both visible strands have within-leg pole-uniformity; when a third position with per-leg pole-mixing exists, the cluster's pole-space is *spectrum-with-bridges*, not a bistable pair.

### Buterin's explicit cluster-bridging

> "If you are an e/acc, then d/acc is a subspecies of e/acc - just one that is much more selective and intentional." (l. 1171-1172)

> "If you are an effective altruist, then d/acc is a re-branding of the effective-altruist idea of differential technology development, though with a greater emphasis on liberal and democratic values." (l. 1173-1175)

The eight-position compatibility list (e/acc, EA, libertarian, Pluralist, public-health, blockchain, solarpunk, lunarpunk) is the cluster-bridging function operationalized at maximum legibility — d/acc claims compatibility with every adjacent subculture rather than distinguishing itself.
