## The reading

**The bead.** Seven outcast kids who have nothing but each other face a thing that eats children, and the weapon that actually works is the bond between them.

**Engines**
- **belonging** · content · spine · ~ — the Losers' Club: the found-family of bullied, stuttering, outcast kids whose loyalty to one another is literally the force that defeats It. Belonging is load-bearing — the monster feeds on isolation and fear, and the cure is the pack that refuses to let each member face it alone.
- **security/safety** · content · also-runs · ~ — It is a predatory threat to children specifically; the wish is to make the town (and childhood itself) safe, the defense mounted at terrible cost across two timelines.

**The bundle.** A belonging spine wrapped in a survival-horror threat — the found-family as the thing that beats the dark, with the safety wish as its stakes.

**Dual-use read.** Belonging's counterfeit is the cult/gang "we are your *real* family now" recruitment — submersion in the group as the price of acceptance. King's version is the enabling pole: the Losers are equals, the loyalty is chosen and costly, and no one is asked to surrender the self to belong.

**Verdict.** A belonging specimen at horror scale — the childhood found-family rendered as the only thing strong enough to face what hunts them.
